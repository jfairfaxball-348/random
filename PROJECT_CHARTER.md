# Project Charter

## Working title

**Fairfax-Ball Randomness Research Programme**

Repository name: `random` (the repository may be renamed later by explicit owner decision).

## Root objective

Investigate the mathematical foundations of genuine randomness deeply enough to determine whether there exists an original, natural and noteworthy notion of randomness that merits a distinct definition and theorem package. If such a notion is found and survives prior-art and mathematical scrutiny, aim to place the definition and associated results in the public mathematical record under the name **Fairfax-Ball Randomness**.

## Scope

The programme may ultimately touch algorithmic randomness, computability, algorithmic information, effective measure/category, probability, logic, randomness tests, martingales, complexity characterizations, dynamical or number-theoretic interfaces, or other adjacent areas. This list is architectural only; Phase 1 determines the actual research map.

## Non-goals

- Inventing a definition merely to attach a name to it.
- Rebranding an existing concept.
- Treating novelty as established by web-search absence.
- Skipping directly to proof search before the literature map and novelty gates.
- Using formal verification as a substitute for prior-art work.
- Claiming publication, peer review or community acceptance before it occurs.

## Success hierarchy

The strongest intended endpoint is:
1. a natural definition or equivalent characterization deserving a distinct name;
2. a rigorous, nontrivial theorem package;
3. convincing differentiation from prior notions;
4. clear mathematical motivation/use;
5. public arXiv dissemination;
6. independent peer-reviewed journal publication.

A scientifically valid negative conclusion at any earlier gate is also a legitimate project outcome.

## Governance

The repository is authoritative. Five phase gates control progress. Gate failure may:
- send work back within the same phase;
- backtrack to an earlier phase;
- retire a candidate;
- or terminate the programme.

All such decisions are logged.
