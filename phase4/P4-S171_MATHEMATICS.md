## A171.1 — Fully effective output savings bank for the S011 F2 destroyer

Fix the ACTUAL existing S011 adaptive fresh-coordinate sentinel scan F and its computably random, wtt-autoreducible witness Y. Along y=F(Y), there are infinitely many sentinel queries and every sentinel prediction is correct; y is nonCR. The scan's finite transcript determines whether the next query is filler or sentinel, and in the latter case the predicted bit, by a total finite computation (S011).

For EACH output prefix tau, define two total integer functions b(tau) >=0 and a(tau) in {0,1}. Initially b(empty)=0, a(empty)=1. On a filler query, both children retain b and a. At a sentinel query with prediction v:
- if a(tau)=1, the v-child sets (b+1,1), the (1-v)-child sets (b,0);
- if a(tau)=0, both children retain (b,0).
Set d_sav(tau)=b(tau)+a(tau). Then d_sav is TOTAL, exactly rational, nonnegative, with d_sav(empty)=1. At an active sentinel its child capitals are b+2 and b, whose arithmetic mean is b+1; at all other nodes both child capitals equal the parent. So d_sav is a martingale on EVERY output sibling, including halted/nontriggering transcripts. b is nondecreasing under prefix extension, is bounded pointwise by d_sav, and along the SAME y=F(Y) increments once in each successful sentinel epoch, hence b(y|n) tends to infinity. No assertion is made that b diverges off that spine.

Put b_n(z)=b(z|n), and beta_n = int b_n d(lambda) = 2^-n sum_{tau in 2^n} b(tau), an EXACT computable rational. Since b_n <= d_sav,n and int d_sav,n=1, 0<=beta_n<=1. The sequence beta_n is computable, nondecreasing and converges to a left-c.e. real alpha in [0,1]. The next theorem proves alpha is **not computable**. This is a concrete infinite-stage price obstruction, not a priori a global-sheet-count conclusion.

## B171.1 — Effective expected-bank mass yields ONE exact rational source winner

THEOREM (scoped, internally proved). Let G:X->X be ANY TOTAL computable lambda-preserving map (no finite-fibre restriction). Let b_n:X->Q_nonnegative be uniformly computable finite-output-prefix cylinder functions, b_n(z) <= b_(n+1)(z) for all z, and let beta_n=int b_n d(lambda) be uniformly computable rationals bounded by some finite value. Suppose alpha=sup_n beta_n is a computable real. Define f(z)=sup_n b_n(z) (extended nonnegative, finite a.e.). Then uniformly from G, b_n and a computable real name for alpha, ONE total computable nonnegative exact RATIONAL source martingale e can be constructed such that for EVERY source x, if sup_n b_n(G(x)) is infinite, e succeeds on THAT SAME x.

PROOF. Let u(n) be a computable modulus of uniform continuity for G's first n output bits. For input prefix s, define
h_n(s) = 2^|s| int_[s] b_n(G(z)) d(lambda)(z).
Choose input length L >= max(|s|,u(n)); enumerate all length-L source words extending s, evaluate G(z)|n and b_n on that prefix, and average. This gives an EXACT rational h_n(s) uniformly in n,s. Each h_n is a source martingale on ALL input siblings, with h_n(empty)=beta_n. Since b_n increases, so does h_n(s). Put h(s)=sup_n h_n(s). By monotone convergence, h(s) is finite (at most 2^|s| alpha) and satisfies h(s0)+h(s1)=2h(s) EXACTLY.

The effective tail bound
0 <= h(s)-h_n(s) <= 2^|s| (alpha-beta_n)
holds because the integrand f-b_n is nonnegative and the input integral over [s] is bounded by the full input integral. Global fairness of G makes the latter equal int(f-b_n) d(lambda)=alpha-beta_n. Given s and requested precision, computability of alpha and the increasing rational beta_n allow us to find n making this upper bound arbitrarily small. Thus h is a uniformly computable REAL, nonnegative source martingale.

Here is explicit exact-rational conversion without an unproved rationalisation step. Choose a positive rational A>h(empty)+1. Recursively at a source node s of length m compute a rational q_s approximating (h(s0)-h(s1))/2 with error <=2^(-m-4). Define e(empty)=A and e(s0)=e(s)+q_s, e(s1)=e(s)-q_s. This is a TOTAL computable EXACT rational martingale: e(s0)+e(s1)=2e(s). Since h has exact sibling equality, the discrepancy e(s)-h(s) equals its initial value A-h(empty)>1 plus signed errors along the path; their total absolute value is at most sum_{m>=0}2^(-m-4)=1/8. Hence e(s)>h(s) and e(s)>=0 on ALL source strings. No positivity or computability is inferred from finite numerical tests.

Fix a source x and output y=G(x). For any n, G's first n output bits are constant with value y|n on the source cone [x|u(n)]. For every t>=u(n), all z in [x|t] therefore satisfy b_m(G(z))>=b_n(y) for m>=n. Thus h(x|t)>=b_n(y) at every such t. If b_n(y) grows without bound, h(x|t), and hence e(x|t), tends to infinity. One fixed e handles all n: no time-varying bettor and no switch of source. QED.

The theorem is a genuinely CHECKABLE sufficient hypothesis if an effective modulus for alpha is supplied, and works for arbitrary source use and ALL output siblings. It does NOT derive that hypothesis from finite complete fibres, nor use B170 branch-prefix advice to claim an inverse kernel. It is related to S160's effective-L1 terminal density transfer, but concerns a monotone **saved bank** rather than a winning martingale's conewise terminal density. It is also compatible with S164's rationalisation of computable-real martingales. No priority claim.

## C171.1 — S011's exact saved-bank expectation is noncomputable

THEOREM (internally proved). For the specific G=F and b_n from A171.1, alpha=sup_n int b_n d(lambda) is left-c.e. but NOT computable.

PROOF. A171.1 gives b_n(F(Y)) increasing without bound for the SAME fixed computably random Y, while F is TOTAL computable globally fair. If alpha were computable, all hypotheses of B171.1 would hold. The resulting ONE TOTAL exact rational source martingale e would succeed on Y, contradicting Y in CR. Therefore alpha is not computable. Its increasing sequence of EXACT rational approximants beta_n supplies the left-c.e. name. QED.

COROLLARY (quantified failure of a proposed compiler). Any claim that for EVERY F_k observer and EVERY such rational monotone winning output bank the finite expected saved capital has a computable limit is FALSE ALREADY FOR k=2, even at a target y with **singleton actual fibre** (S011). In this witness no computable schedule N(e) can guarantee alpha-beta_{N(e)} <= 2^-e for all e; such a schedule would make alpha computable. This is a noncomputability of one SPECIFIED bank's terminal expectation, NOT a claim that every other possible capital transfer is impossible, that finite inverse advice is uniformly unpriceable, or that H1 fails.

A companion useful exact all-sibling criterion requires no alpha: let H_n(s)=h_n(s) as above. If a TOTAL computable nonnegative rational source SUPERMARTINGALE E(s), of finite root value, majorises H_n(s) for ALL source strings s and ALL n, then E succeeds on each x with unbounded b_n(G(x)); indeed after source use u(n), E(x|t)>=b_n(G(x)) permanently. The exact rational martingale D dominating E is obtained by D(empty)=E(empty), D(s0)=D(s)+(E(s0)-E(s1))/2 and D(s1)=D(s)-(E(s0)-E(s1))/2; induction and E(s)>=(E(s0)+E(s1))/2 give D(s)>=E(s)>=0. For S011 NO such computable finite-root all-n envelope can exist, else Y would be nonCR. This is an explicit falsifier for proposed finite-sheet budget proofs; the envelope is a strong ADDITIONAL HYPOTHESIS, not forced by the cap.

## D171.1 — Targetwise F1 completion of S170's nonextendible a.e. map

Retain S170's P on D=X\{0^omega}: P(0^n1bz)=b0^n1z. For EACH x=0^n1bz in D fix length L=n+2 and distinct or equal length-L words s=0^n1b, t=b0^n1. Select a computable finite PERMUTATION pi of ALL words of length L with pi(s)=t (e.g. the transposition s<->t if distinct, otherwise identity). Define H_x(wz)=pi(w)z for every w of length L and tail z. Then H_x is TOTAL computable, lambda-preserving on EVERY cylinder, a homeomorphism with computable inverse, and has exactly ONE complete inverse for EVERY output. It belongs to F1 and satisfies H_x(sz)=tz=P(sz) on the ENTIRE input cylinder [s], hence H_x(x)=P(x).

Quantifiers: for EACH particular x in D, existence of one finite hardcoded chart π is enough; the chart can also be selected by a partial computable procedure with oracle x on D by waiting for its first 1 and next bit. This does NOT produce one TOTAL map extending P on all CR inputs: S170 proves THAT impossible. Further P itself preserves CR (finite prefix rearrangement), so this targetwise F1 replacement is a NON-SEPARATOR and proves neither positive nor negative H1. It does show that failure of a global continuous extension does NOT prevent target-specific total legal replacement when the a.e. map has a finite equal-weight chart.
