## A147.1 — Stopping-price non-effectivity persists in every source cone

**PROPOSITION (INTERNALLY PROVED, scoped corollary of S145; arbitrary computable use).** Let \(G:2^\omega\to2^\omega\) be total computable and globally fair; let \(d\) be a nonnegative total rational computable output martingale with root 1. Write \(E_k=\{y:\exists n\ d(y\upharpoonright n)\ge 2^{4k}\}\), \(U_k=G^{-1}E_k\). Suppose \(x\in CR\) and \(\sup_n d(G(x)\upharpoonright n)=\infty\). For **every** finite nonempty computably clopen \(B\ni x\) and **every** total computable strictly increasing function \(j\mapsto k_j\), the real sequence
\[
 p^B_j=\lambda(B\cap U_{k_j})
\]
is **NOT uniformly computable as a real sequence**. The same conclusion holds for the conditional local prices \(p^B_j/\lambda(B)\), since \(\lambda(B)>0\) is a known rational. No claim that every individual \(p^B_j\) is noncomputable is made.

**Proof.** \(B\cap U_{k_j}\) is uniformly c.e. open: a fixed clopen \(B\), total computability of \(G\), and a total rational \(d\) suffice. By global fairness and Ville's inequality, \(\lambda(B\cap U_{k_j})\le\lambda(U_{k_j})=\lambda(E_{k_j})\le 2^{-4k_j}\le 2^{-4j}\), after indexing \(j\ge1\). If the exact local real prices were uniformly computable, S145 A145.1 applied to the uniformly c.e.-open sets \(V_j=B\cap U_{k_j}\) would produce ONE total rational source martingale succeeding on every sequence in infinitely many \(V_j\). But \(x\in B\) and \(d\) is unbounded on \(G(x)\), so \(x\in V_j\) for **all** \(j\). Contradiction to CR. Conditional division by the positive computable rational \(\lambda(B)\) preserves uniform real computability both ways. QED.

**Quantifier force.** \(B\) and the computable subsequence may be chosen separately, with no uniform operation on oracle \(x\); each alleged local computable-price chart already produces a contradiction. This is stronger spatial information than just the failure of uniform global \(\lambda(E_k)\) prices in S145 but is a direct corollary, not an independent theorem about \(R_2/R_3\). It requires no restriction on complete fibres and therefore also applies to the genuine S011 \(G\in\mathcal F_2\) destroyer \(Y\in CR\), \(G(Y)\notin CR\). In that example every cylinder containing \(Y\) has hard *uniform* stopping prices. Any proposed argument that 'three sheets have noncomputable local prices, two sheets do not' is thereby disproved.

**Atlas corollary (SUFFICIENT, NOT established for every G).** If for a fixed fair \(G,d\) there is a countable family of computable clopen sets \(B_i\) covering a class \(C\), with for each \(i\) uniformly computable exact \(\lambda(B_i\cap U_{k_j})\) along some computable unbounded threshold subsequence (which may depend on \(i\)), then no computably random \(x\in C\) can have \(d(G(x))\) unbounded. ## B147.1 — Sharp two-step wager frontier at the forced 1→2 transition

For a fair additive-use-two map \(G\) with exactly TWO actual inverse points \(x_A,x_B\) above a fixed \(y\), choose disjoint clopen isolating cones \(A,B\). After non-effectively late stabilization each output \(m\)-cell has exactly four aligned source \((m+2)\)-cells, distributed \((q_A,4-q_A)\), with \(q_A\in\{1,2,3\}\). The seven legal target \((q_A,t_A)\) transitions are \((1,1),(1,2),(2,1),(2,2),(2,3),(3,2),(3,3)\); exact labelled row counts \(40,15,16,36,16,15,40\), total 178, as in S146. This is NOT a count of full globally legal observers.

At a \(q_A=1\to2\) transition BOTH source children of the sole \(A\)-cell \(s\) follow the chosen first output bit. One further output bit selects exactly \(t\in\{1,2,3\}\) of the FOUR length-two source grandchildren in \(A\), with the other \(4-t\) assigned to the sibling output child. In the **omniscient finite-block experiment**, in which the desired second output bit and hence its finite leaf set are supplied beforehand, the maximal capital factor that an ordinary source martingale starting with 1 at \(s\) can guarantee on EVERY winning target grandchild is exactly \(4/t\).

**Proof of upper bound.** For any nonnegative ordinary martingale \(M\) starting at \(M(s)=1\), the four grandchildren's capitals sum to 4. If each of the \(t\) target leaves has capital \(\ge a\), then \(ta\le4\). **Attainment:** set capital \(4/t\) at exactly the \(t\) selected leaves and 0 on the others; define the two intermediate child capitals by backward dyadic averages. This gives a nonnegative rational fair source martingale on the finite two-step binary tree. Thus the factor \(4/t\) is exact.

**Sharp complementary-fallback budget.** If one wishes to guarantee at least \(a\) on EVERY target grandchild and at least \(b\) on EVERY sibling grandchild, the necessary and sufficient finite-tree feasibility condition is
\[
                   t\,a+(4-t)\,b\ \le\ 4. \tag{B147}
\]
Necessity follows from the same sum. Sufficiency follows by giving the designated leaves their floors \(a,b\), allocating any remaining capital nonnegatively, and computing parent averages. Therefore it is IMPOSSIBLE to guarantee **both** \(a>1\) and \(b>1\) at this finite step. In particular choosing the optimal target-only factor \(4/t\) requires zero sibling reserve \(b=0\). For \(t=3\) the target gain can be as small as \(4/3\), while the other continuation could carry all loss. The symmetric \(q_A=3\to2\) case applies to the sole \(B\)-cell.

**Why this does not compile E-08.** A source observer standing at \(s\) is NOT given the next actual output bit in advance; along \(x\) it is revealed only through later source bits. The 'target-only' account is a **future-output-oracle betting certificate**, not one algorithmic source martingale that can select the correct target branch uniformly. Allocating the full bankroll to both incompatible target/sibling continuations violates (B147). A diversified source strategy may still eventually win via delayed escrow, global correlations or an altogether DIFFERENT F2/e; (B147) does not exclude it. Nor can finite row assignments certify that a proposed F2 has ≤2 **complete infinite** inverses above ALL outputs or that its output measure is fair. The S135 globally fair 3:1↔2:2 cycle and S141 benign positive-fold pockets remain hostile controls.
