## Definitions and mathematical objective

μ_B(V)=λ(B∩G⁻¹(V))/λ(B), the output distribution conditional on B.
For y write deg_B(y)=|B∩G⁻¹{y}|. μ_B is a computable probability measure, actually rational on each cylinder: B and G⁻¹[τ] are effectively clopen, and λ(B) is a strictly positive dyadic rational. ## Theorem A141.1 (proved internally, original Rute E-ISO reused)
**If μ_B({y:deg_B(y)=1})=1, then ∀x∈CR∩B, G(x)∈CR.** No additive-use bound, global fibre cap, computable inverse-decoding modulus, onto range for G|B, or global conull-singleton assumption is required.

Proof:
1. Use the **computable conditional input measure** λ_B(A)=λ(A∩B)/λ(B) on the whole Cantor space, including its zero-mass cylinders outside B. For x∈CR∩B, x is CR relative to λ_B: take finite N resolving B into N-cylinders and σ=x↾N∈B. Any rational λ_B-martingale that wins on x restricts below σ to an ordinary fair-coin rational martingale, because for all descendants η⊇σ, λ_B[η]=λ[η]/λ(B). Extend this winning subtree to a globally total ordinary λ martingale by setting zero on all other N-cylinders and taking parent averages; it wins on x, contradiction. (For computable-real wager conventions use standard rational equivalence as in S101/Rute §2.) Thus x is λ_B-computably random without falsely assuming that an arbitrary dyadic measure λ(B) is a power of two. Let T=G as a map (X,λ_B)→(X,μ_B); μ_B is its computable pushforward measure.
2. For each finite τ and source precision m, finite-oracle totality of G gives an effective common use bound and an exact finite set of m-bit source cones **intersecting B** and compatible with B∩G⁻¹[τ]. On any output y with precisely one inverse *inside B*, these compatibility sets eventually become singleton for every m by compactness. Their uniform search defines a partial inverse J on an effective Π⁰₂ singleton-fibre domain D with μ_B(D)=1. The inverse identities hold λ_B- and μ_B-a.e., with J transporting μ_B back to λ_B (NOT unconditioned λ). Rute arXiv:1203.5535v4, Definitions 7.1/7.5, Proposition 7.6, Proposition 7.8 and Theorem 7.11 now imply that λ_B-computably random x has μ_B-computably random image G(x). Both computable measures may have zero-mass cylinders; Rute §2 explicitly addresses them. No point outside B is required to lie in the inverse identity domain.
3. Verify the last step to ordinary *fair-coin* CR explicitly rather than silently replacing μ_B by λ. Suppose a total rational fair-coin martingale d succeeds on y=T(z). Its associated finite rational measure ν has ν[τ]=2⁻|τ|d(τ). At μ_B[τ]>0 set h(τ)=ν[τ]/μ_B[τ] (all rational); h is a nonnegative μ_B-supermartingale because μ_B-zero children can lose ν mass. To make a TOTAL rational μ_B-*martingale* m dominating h on μ_B-positive cylinders, put m(ε)=h(ε) and recursively, whenever μ_B[τ]>0, set δ_τ=ν[τ]−Σ_{b:μ_B[τb]>0}ν[τb]≥0 and m(τb)=h(τb)+(m(τ)−h(τ))+δ_τ/μ_B[τ] for each μ_B-positive child; set m=0 below a μ_B-zero node. ## Corollary A141.2 (positive *joint* internal folding is necessary)

## Theorem B141.1 (fully global legal false-positive construction; internally proved)

(a) P(x)=x and deg_P(x)=1 for every x∈K;
(b) deg_P(y)=2 for every y outside K;
(c) for every x∈K and every prefixes s≺x and τ≺P(x), B(s,τ) contains a positive-measure clopen pocket on which P has two full inverses, hence the A141.2 and S140 positive-neighbourhood demands both hold;

Construction and proof:
1. Enumerate all finite binary words σ_j in length-lex order, j≥0. Recursively, if [σ_j] is already wholly inside the finite union of previously selected disjoint pocket cylinders, skip. Otherwise choose effectively the lexicographically first remaining cylinder [ρ_j]⊂[σ_j] disjoint from all previous pockets with |ρ_j|≥max(j+4,|σ_j|+1). It exists because the finite leftover in [σ_j] is nonempty clopen. Reindex only for notation if skipping; stage-bound |ρ_j|≥j+4 remains. The pockets form a prefix-free uniformly computable list; their union O is c.e. open and DENSE: every σ_j either was covered or contains its new pocket. Σ_j 2⁻|ρ_j|≤Σ_j2⁻(j+4)=1/8. Therefore K=X\O is effective Π⁰₁ closed, nowhere dense, λ(K)≥7/8>0.
2. Define P(ρ_j b u)=ρ_j u for each pocket and b∈{0,1}; define P(x)=x on K. No pockets overlap, and each pocket is mapped ONTO ITSELF by deleting its first post-pocket bit. P is computable with output use ≤n+1 to determine its first n output bits: only pockets of length <n can affect those bits; those have stage j<n and can be finitely enumerated. A pocket of length ≥n leaves all first n bits unchanged, so deciding membership in K is **never needed**. Thus global cap TWO and all-output total legality are explicit. If x∈K, P(x)=x. Every [s] containing x contains some entire pocket [ρ_j] (density of O and x∉O). For every τ≺x choose a pocket inside [s]∩[τ]; it lies inside B(s,τ), and its positive input/output mass is doubled. Hence the necessary source/output local fold tests are satisfied at x.
