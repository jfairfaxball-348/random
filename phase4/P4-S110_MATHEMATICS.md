## II. CONSTRUCTION — a family with genuinely infinitely recurring 48-type hybrid four-state tables

The hidden initial key a is never emitted.

**Theorem A (PROVED INTERNALLY).** For ALL such total computable t, G_t is everywhere total computable, exactly fair, of original-coordinate additive use at most m+2, and has EXACTLY TWO distinct actual inverse sequences for EVERY output y. Indeed conditional on key a=1 the unique inverse is (1,y). Conditional on a=0, given y decode its blocks in order: e=t(y|2j) is already known and uniquely determines whether to unswap the current output pair. The inverse (0,P_0^-1(y)) is total computable and distinct from the key-one inverse. No third point exists because the first bit selects one of these two deterministic algorithms. Conditional on either key, each 2-bit source block is an independent uniform fair block and the adaptive step is a permutation of its four possibilities; inductively every output cylinder, including an odd-depth cylinder, has exact dyadic measure 2^-m. Since the keys have equal price, the mixture is fair. At every output depth m there are exactly FOUR original source prefixes of length m+2 (two per key) above each output m-word. ## III. CONSERVATION — explicit ordinary rational 2-bit source martingale

**Theorem C (PROVED INTERNALLY).** EVERY G_t preserves CR at EVERY source, despite its unsaturated four-prefix candidates and arbitrarily many 48-type hybrids. Proof avoids a general conditional-measure or source-fibre selector.

Suppose y=G_t(a,u) and d is an ordinary total nonnegative rational computable output martingale succeeding on y. Hard-code the actual key a, a legitimate fixed finite parameter for constructing a source martingale. For even-length tail input v, define E_a(v)=d(P_a(v)), where P_a(v) is the fully computed output on complete 2-bit blocks. At odd length v, define E_a(v)=(E_a(v0)+E_a(v1))/2 from its even-length children. At every even v, the four source 2-bit extensions are mapped bijectively onto the four output 2-bit extensions by a permutation controlled by the previous output prefix. Thus the average of their d-values is d(P_a(v)), so E_a is a TOTAL nonnegative rational ordinary source martingale at odd AND even levels.

A nonnegative martingale can grow by at most a factor of two in one step. Thus if output d is unbounded along y but bounded at all even lengths, its odd values would also be bounded: contradiction. Consequently d has unbounded values at even lengths, where E_a(u|2j)=d(y|2j). Extend E_a to a full-source martingale by setting D(empty)=d(empty), giving both key children root value d(empty), following E_a on the chosen key cone and remaining constant on the other cone. D is total computable rational, nonnegative and succeeds at (a,u), contradicting its CR. No noncomputable stabilization time, stopping price, hidden conditional-mass oracle or arbitrary limit is used.

The schedule t=0 makes G_t(a,u)=u, deletion of a fixed first bit. CR is invariant under adding/removing a *fixed finite initial bit* by conditional martingale restriction.
