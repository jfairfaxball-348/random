## A124-1: ONE RATIONAL martingale handles every finite unknown common-tail shift

    T_(k,l)(s)= 1                  when |s|<=k;

With p_k=2^(-k-1),q_l=2^(-l-1), define D(s)=sum_(k,l>=0) p_k q_l T_(k,l)(s), an ordinary root-one nonnegative COMPUTABLE-REAL martingale.
