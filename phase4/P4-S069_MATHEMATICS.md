## 3. A fixed-M exceptional target-locus barrier for measure/category transfers

\[

**Theorem 4 (fixed-program exceptional autoreduction locus).**
\[
\boxed{\quad \mu(E_M)=\mu(E_X)=0,\qquad
E_M\text{ and }E_X\text{ are meagre}.\quad}
\tag{8}
\]
*Measure proof.* For each q let tau_q flip only virtual coordinate q. Since M(q) NEVER queries q on ANY oracle (syntactic avoidance), the runs on z and tau_q(z) on input q agree. They cannot both output their opposite q-bits correctly, so E_M and tau_q(E_M) are disjoint. Let C be any finite union of cylinders approximating E_M in fair measure. For every sufficiently large q, tau_q(C)=C. Thus
\[
\mu(E_M\mathbin{\triangle}\tau_q E_M)
\le2\mu(E_M\mathbin{\triangle}C).
\]
The left side equals 2 mu(E_M) by disjointness, whereas the right side can be made arbitrarily small by measurable-cylinder approximation. Therefore mu(E_M)=0. H is a fair-coin-preserving computable homeomorphism, so mu(E_X)=0. *Category proof.* E_M, as a G_delta, has the Baire property. If it were nonmeagre, it would be comeagre in some nonempty cylinder C. Pick q beyond the coordinates defining C; tau_q fixes C and carries E_M to another comeagre subset of C, which must intersect E_M, contradicting disjointness. H is a homeomorphism, so E_X is meagre.
