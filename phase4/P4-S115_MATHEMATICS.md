## 1. Hostile S114 audit: actual pairs and finite automata

Given a finite-state synchronous transducer with c input bits buffered, at output depth m a pair of runs consumes m+c input bits. Retain BOTH c-bit initial histories, both internal control states, and all first-r discrepancy information until at least r input bits have been consumed. For short m with m+c<r, do NOT reject an equal-so-far pair: the two source continuations may first differ before r. Once r bits have been consumed, discard pairs whose first r bits agree, and thereafter remember the nonzero split witness even if the finite control states coalesce. A pair of identical control states after distinct first-r histories is a legitimate pair of distinct sources with a common continuation.

Let P_m(y) be the finite set of surviving length-(m+c) paired histories consistent with y|m and differing before r (where for m+c<r keep extendible pending discrepancy states). The finite pair tree is prefix-closed after the r-phase. Nonempty states at all sufficiently long output depths imply arbitrarily long consistent split pairs; finite branching and König compactness yield two ACTUAL infinite inverses. Conversely a pair of real inverses gives a branch at all stages. Individual finite candidate states can die; no finite collision, even repeatedly observed, is an infinite inverse. One may prune pending equal histories only after r bits have been read; that initial-phase caveat is essential.

The deterministic powerset of finite paired-control-plus-phase states is a finite safety automaton. A nondead state is SAFE if dead is unreachable via any future word; this is finite reachability. The undecided subgraph has no closed recurrent communicating class: every undecided state can reach dead. Let N>=1 be the total number of powerset states and take N steps per block; a path to dead of length <=N occurs with fair conditional probability at least 2^(-N) from any undecided state. Hence P(undecided after kN) <=(1-2^(-N))^k. Infinite unresolved paths are an effectively closed null set, NOT necessarily empty. The fair mass of the safety language is rational: let Q be the rational substochastic matrix on undecided states and b the one-step probability of reaching SAFE. The unique vector of eventual SAFE-hitting probabilities solves (I-Q)p=b; invertibility follows from transience. Treat starting dead/safe separately. This computes lambda(D_r) exactly even for pairs with zero or positive limiting sheet weights.

## 3. Front A/C — a fully legal nonregular exact-two test with PERFECT effective-null ramification boundary

New explicit input example, expanding S114's finite-state frontier without claiming a CR destroyer. Define special zero-indexed positions p_k=2^k-1 for k=0,1,2,... (0,1,3,7,...). For infinite output tail u, let k be the least special index with u(p_k)=1, if any. Define K as: if such k exists, accept u iff k is EVEN; if no such k exists, accept u. The prefix tree T_K is decidable: for a finite word w inspect only p_k<|w|, accept if no special-position 1 is yet seen or the FIRST such k is even. It is prefix-closed, with infinite path set exactly K.

Let E={u: u(p_k)=0 for EVERY k}. This is an effectively closed PERFECT null set: the infinitely many nonspecial coordinates remain free, and the finite restriction to the first h special positions has fair mass 2^(-h). The first-hit cones at even k are clopen and constitute int(K); first-hit cones at odd k are clopen complement(K). The only boundary points are E. In particular
    lambda(K)=sum_{even k>=0} 2^(-k-1)=2/3,
    boundary(K)=E, lambda(E)=0.
Neither K nor E is regular omega-language in a finite-state sense. For instance u_n=0^n 1 0^infinity is rejected exactly when n=p_k for an ODD k (the other n either first-hit at an even special coordinate or remain E). An omega-regular automaton on inputs 0^n10^infinity eventually repeats its state after zeros, so its acceptance set in n must be ultimately periodic. The rejecting n=2^(2j+1)-1 are infinite with unbounded gaps, contradicting ultimate periodicity. Thus K is not omega-regular safety. This is a genuine infinite-control example, not a finite gadget.
