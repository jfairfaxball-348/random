## 1. Exact proposed bridge and mechanism (stated before subsidiary proofs)

Fix a computable index e for a uniformly c.e.-open ordinary ML test `(U^e_j)`, with `lambda(U^e_j)<=2^{-j}`. The index supplies a finite-stage enumeration algorithm, NOT computable membership/measure, a true-path clock, or a chosen source. Write CR and MLR in the S101 rational-martingale/test conventions. Use the **frozen S083** fixed map `F=F_{T*} o D'` and rational output martingale `d*`. Its established properties are: total computable fair F; every output has at most two inverse points; some `z* in CR\MLR` has `d*(F(z*)↾t)` unbounded; and any distinct F-fibre pair differs by the raw tail-complement vector `1_[q,infinity)` for a finite q (S083 §4.3; S084 §4). We do NOT replace F by a deleted-bit map.

For a finite integer a>=0 and block j, and a *raw* prefix p of length 2j, define computably:
`b_{e,a}(j,p)=1` when j is EVEN. For ODD j put `b=1` iff by enumeration stage `j+a` some already emitted cylinder `[sigma]` for `U^e_j` satisfies `|sigma|<=2j` and `sigma preceq p`; otherwise 0. Simulate precisely finitely many enumeration steps. No negative Π⁰₁ oracle or last-emission oracle appears.

Define the recursive two-bit source shear `K_{e,a}`, block j, with original prefix p=x↾2j:
`K(x)_{2j}=x_{2j} XOR (b_{e,a}(j,p) AND x_{2j+1})`,
`K(x)_{2j+1}=x_{2j+1}`.
Let `G_{e,*}=F` and `G_{e,a}=F o K_{e,a}`. These induce **different source dyadic partitions** `P^{e,a}_w=G_{e,a}^{-1}[w]`; membership-triggered shears at odd blocks really depend on raw prior bits, though no source oracle is used in the algorithm.

The proposed **CB104-fast coverage bridge**, with its exact and deliberately testable effective clause, is:
`forall e [U^e an ML test] forall z in CR intersect (intersection_j U^e_j), exists b in {*} union N, exists total computable rational nonnegative output martingale d with d(empty)>0, exists total computable g:N->N, exists N, forall n>=N, exists t<=g(n): d(G_{e,b}(z)↾t) >= 2^{n+2} d(empty).`
The finite integers b,N and algorithms for d,g may be selected EXISTENTIALLY depending on e,z, but must each have ordinary computable indices, not z-oracle indices. ## 2. Global legality and searchable width geometry

**Lemma 2.1 (actual map legality, not target-only).** Every K_{e,a} is an everywhere-total computable measure-preserving Cantor homeomorphism. On input x, a block decision only uses x↾2j and a finite enumeration prefix; its two-bit map is a bijection of {0,1}² for each fixed previous prefix. Decode K(x) block-by-block: earlier raw blocks are already recovered, so the same b is recomputed and the odd then even bit recovered. This gives a total computable inverse on ALL sequences. Inductively each conditioned fresh block remains uniformly distributed, proving global fair measure preservation; equivalently each length-2j block-prefix has exact mass 2^{-2j}. Thus each G_{e,a} is total computable and fair, and every fibre has at most two members because K_{e,a} is bijective and F has that bound on EVERY output. F itself is also legal.

**Lemma 2.2 (cells and raw-prefix search).** For every b and output word w, the preimage P^b_w is a uniformly computable **clopen finite prefix-code** set, using the effective preimage procedure for total computable Cantor maps (S003/S085). `P_empty=2^omega`, `P_w=P_{w0} disjoint-union P_{w1}`, and `lambda(P_w)=2^{-|w|}`. At raw precision n and depth m compute all finite overlaps `L(n,w)={sigma in 2^n:[sigma] intersect P_w !=empty}`. Search successive m until *every* word w of length m has |L(n,w)|<=2. This is a fully specified total algorithm: if it did not halt, the finitely branching tree of cells meeting >=3 distinct raw n-cylinders would have an infinite path; nested finite prefix lists and compactness would yield 3 distinct points of one G-fibre. Contradiction to Lemma 2.1. Conversely such a searchable bound alone gives only two inverse n-prefix candidates, NOT a computable coherent choice of sheet or a success transfer (S003/S007). All outputs, all raw precisions, and all source paths are covered.

**Lemma 2.3 (genuine two-channel source incompatibility).** For each e,a, the non-diagonal fibre equivalence relations of F and F o K_{e,a} are disjoint. If F(x)=F(x') with x≠x', then x XOR x' is 1_[q,infinity) (S083). At every sufficiently late EVEN block j, the mask of K is forced to 1 on BOTH x and x'; their raw block difference is (1,1), while their K-block difference is (0,1). Hence K(x) XOR K(x') is NOT any final tail 1_[q',infinity), so F(Kx)≠F(Kx'). This applies to every pair, including any exceptional output: on singleton fibres the premise has no pair.

If F=Q_0 o T and F o K=Q_1 o T with T an everywhere-total computable FAIR map and Q_i each preserving CR on all CR inputs, any T-double pair would be a double pair for both observations, contradicting Lemma 2.3. Thus T is injective; compactness, full-support fair pushforward and effective finite-separation make it a computable fair homeomorphism with computable inverse. Q_0 o T would then preserve CR, contradicting the S083 CR-destruction witness for F. **Therefore these two source-side channels have NO common CR-preserving output factor of S086's prohibited kind.** This is a geometry result, not a coverage theorem.

Likewise no G_{e,a} is `pi o L` with pi deleting one fixed input coordinate and L a computable fair homeomorphism: those preserve CR by S003, whereas G_{e,a} destroys the CR point K_{e,a}^{-1}(z*) (see below). Mere output homeomorphic recoding of F has the SAME destruction set and cannot explain distinct kernels.

## 3. Gain, quantifier, partners and false coverage bridge

**Lemma 3.1 (actual, narrowly scoped output gain).** S083 supplies total rational d* succeeding on F(z*). Therefore for each e,a the explicit transported source x_{e,a}=K_{e,a}^{-1}(z*) lies in CR\MLR by effective fair isomorphism, and d* succeeds on G_{e,a}(x_{e,a})=F(z*). This is ONE witness PER observer, not `forall z`; the sources x_{e,a} vary, and z* is not proved caught by every G. The S083 event charging, savings and predictions apply only to its specially built z*. The new shears supply no new source-specific gain lemma for an arbitrary CR nonMLR z.

**Theorem 3.2 (CB104-fast is false, at precisely its quantifiers).** More strongly: for EVERY total computable fair G, every z in CR, every total computable rational nonnegative output martingale d with d(empty)>0 and every total computable g, it is FALSE that for all sufficiently large n the output capital reaches 2^{n+2}d(empty) by time g(n).

Proof. Define `B_n={y: max_{t<=g(n)} d(y↾t)>=2^{n+2}d(empty)}`. It is uniformly finite **clopen** because g and d are total computable and rational. Doob/Ville's finite maximal inequality gives `lambda(B_n)<=2^{-n-2}`. Since G is total computable fair, `A_n=G^{-1}(B_n)` is uniformly computable clopen with exactly computable **rational** measure equal to lambda(B_n). Therefore (A_n) is a Schnorr test (small levels with uniformly computable exact measures). Every CR source is Schnorr random (S099). If the alleged bounded gains held for n>=N, z would lie in the intersection of the tail test (A_{N+k})_k, impossible. No computable schedule is smuggled in: this theorem proves ALL such schedules fail. In particular take the existing z*∈CR\MLR and any universal ML test containing z* on every level; this is an exact counterexample to CB104-fast for every choice of b,d,g,N. ∎

**Repair attempt 1: remove computable gain deadline.** The unbounded bridge `forall e forall z in CR intersect intersection(U_j^e) exists b,d with sup_t d(G_{e,b}(z)↾t)=infinity` would imply EQ if proved using this fixed legal family. It is **NOT proved or refuted here**, and its all-source coverage is at least as hard as the chosen-family restriction of EQ; no independent reduction was established. Theorem 3.2 does NOT say the genuine hitting times are bounded, nor that all fair G preserve CR (S083 is the counterexample).

**Repair attempt 2: demand infinitely many short test tickets.** This alleged trigger property is also false *as a property of uniformly c.e.-open ML tests*. Given any e, replace each enumerated cylinder [sigma] for U_j by the finite collection of its extensions at length `max(|sigma|,2j+1)`. The resulting uniformly c.e. presentation e' defines **exactly the same U_j and measures** but emits NO cylinder of length <=2j. Consequently b_{e',a}(j,p)=0 at EVERY odd j for ALL p,a, while even j remains 1. The entire test-steered family collapses to F and one fixed even-block shear F o K_even. Thus test membership at every level cannot force even one short-ticket event under all valid presentations. This does NOT prove the resulting two maps cannot jointly cover CR\MLR; it invalidates the proposed **trigger justification**, not the relaxed all-source bridge.

**Partners, errors and real source price.** Each distinct pair of a G_{e,a}-fibre is K^{-1} of an F-pair, hence differs by the computable homeomorphism K^{-1} o J_q o K for a finite q. Effective fair homeomorphisms conserve MLR in both directions: when one partner is nonMLR, so is the other. Thus a universal ordinary ML test can enumerate certificates for BOTH; it provides neither an effective orientation nor a favourable entry race. S084 says that on any CR output an infinite computably selected predictable error stream is CR and has infinitely many errors. No all-correct or finitely wrong guess assumption was proved for arbitrary source; an output martingale with variable stakes/savings/restarts still needs ACTUAL output-visible gain. Finite source tickets are not a priced infinite gain sequence. Theorem 3.2 prices every computably timed attempted gain by a uniformly clopen source event, excluding CR paths; noncomputably late gains remain possible and necessary for any future successful EQ approach.
