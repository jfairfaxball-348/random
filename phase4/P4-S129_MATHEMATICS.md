## A. Universal one-surplus source theorem

**A129-1 (source volatility obstruction).** Let c>=2 and G be ANY total computable fair additive-c map. At an output y suppose the ACTUAL fibre has exactly r=N-1 points. Choose equal-length disjoint cylinders B_1,...,B_r isolating those points. If the identity of the doubled B-cone changes infinitely often in the eventual (2,1,...,1) occupancy vector above y, EVERY actual inverse is nonCR. This is a SOURCE statement, not a pullback of the old output volatility martingale. G need not globally have cap N-1; the exact fibre hypothesis is at y.

Proof of eventual geometry. At all sufficiently late output levels every compatible source cell lies inside the union of the B_i. Otherwise the nested compact inverse sets outside that clopen union would have a point above y, contrary to completeness of the fibre list. Every B_i has a real inverse, hence at least one cell. There are N=r+1 cells in total: exactly one cone has two and every other cone has one. There is no effective stabilization level asserted or needed.

Here is a computable source betting rule for fixed B_i and a fixed delayed start h. At a source word s of length n=m+c, compute its output tau, the N compatible n-cells, and both child output populations at depth n+1. Act only when the parent is entirely in these cones with vector (2,1,...,1), and some child tau b has that vector with its doubled cone j DIFFERENT from the parent's doubled cone. There is at most one such child: the parent j-cone has one cell, and both its source children must go to tau b, leaving zero j-cells in the sibling. This test and b are computable from finite tables, without knowledge of y.

If s is in a cone other than j and EXACTLY one of s0,s1 emits the distinguished output bit b, bet all capital on that source child. Otherwise hold. Every bet has child factors (2,0); every hold (1,1). Thus the account is an ordinary total rational source martingale. Before the delayed start it is identically 1. Along y after eventual geometry begins, each actual inverse in a cone other than the newly doubled cone occupies a parent whose EXACTLY ONE child continues to tau b, since its cone's child population is one. Therefore each such source bet is correct; no eligible actual bet loses.

For any fixed inverse cone i, infinitely many changes of the doubled cone include infinitely many transitions whose NEW cone is not i: transitions arriving at i cannot occur consecutively in the sequence of changes, since another change must first leave i. Thus its true inverse receives infinitely many correct doubling bets. Choose h beyond the finite irregular beginning: the account wins at every true inverse. No rate of changes or last-injury oracle is needed. QED.

**One uniform rational compiler for all isolating advice and delayed starts.** Enumerate all finite tuples consisting of r distinct equal-length binary cylinders and a start h. For tuple i let M_i be the preceding unit account, delayed also until source length >=i+1 and cylinder membership is decided. Set, for |s|=n,

    W_G(s) = sum_(i<n) 2^(-i-1) M_i(s) + 2^(-n).

The unstarted tail i>=n is EXACTLY constant 1, so this is a finite RATIONAL computation, not a real-valued infinite-sum approximation. Each account is a martingale with root 1; new accounts are still 1 at their introduction edge. Hence W_G(s0)+W_G(s1)=2 W_G(s). Some enumerated tuple has the correct isolating cylinders and a late enough start. Its fixed positive coefficient proves W_G wins on EVERY inverse in the infinite-rotation case. Construction is uniform in a program for G and promised additive bound c; it does not decide those promises. It compiles one-surplus SOURCE volatility, not arbitrary output martingale success at the stationary doubled cone.

**A129-2 (at most one CR inverse over a bad output).** Under the same exact N-1 fibre hypothesis, if y is nonCR, at most ONE point of G^-1(y) is CR. Infinite rotation gives none by A129-1. With finitely many changes, r-1 cones eventually have exactly one compatible cell. For an output winner d, put p=d+1. The S117 gated source account copies p(tau b)/p(tau) when the two source children emit different output bits and holds when both emit the same bit. Its child average is exactly one. On an eventual one-cell cone each actual row must be permutive, else two child cells would continue inside that cone. Hence its positive factors telescope to a constant times p(y|m), and it wins. Only the eventual doubled cone remains eligible for a CR inverse. This excludes ALL-three-CR over nonCR for EVERY fair additive-two G with a three-point fibre, not just S128's map. It does not exclude one CR inverse, more overhead, or fibres with two surplus cells.

## B. Local reduction: exact-three additive-two observers cannot separate R2/R3

**B129-1 (one-surplus R2 conservation).** For the G,c,y of A129-1, if x is in R2 and G(x)=y, then y is CR. Consequently every globally everywhere-exact-(2^c-1) fair additive-c observer sends R2 into CR. In particular every globally exact-THREE fair additive-TWO observer conserves R2 in the sense required by its defining CR-image tests. This is NOT G(R2) subset R2 and NOT R2=R3.

Proof. R2 subset CR. Infinite rotation is excluded by A129-1. If x is in one of the eventual single-cell cones, A129-2's compiler excludes a bad image. Otherwise its isolating cone B has exactly TWO compatible source cells over every sufficiently long y-prefix. Choose tau0=y|m0 after this stabilizes and after B membership is decided. Write its two B source cells s0,s1 of length n0=m0+c. Normalize input i u to s_i u, i=0,1. For the actual inverse x=s_i u the normalized source z=i u is in R2: delete the first n0-1 bits of x and flip the surviving first bit, if necessary, to i. This uses S113's finite deletion/flip theorem. No arbitrary prefix INSERTION closure is assumed.

Build a new Q on ALL normalized inputs, with one-bit additive use. It starts with two input candidates 0,1 corresponding to s0,s1. At each output node, test whether their four original source children split exactly 2:2 between the next output bits. If yes, use those rows and retain the two candidates in each output child. If no, permanently switch to emitting fresh chronological normalized tail bits with the current two candidates as a hidden label. Both choices give exactly TWO source (j+1)-cells over EVERY normalized output j-word. Therefore Q is total computable, globally fair, and in F2 by compactness; all fallback siblings are included.

On the target tail after tau0, the B-cell count stays two. Its two child output counts add to four, so actual-child count two forces sibling count two. Thus every actual node passes the finite balance test. No fallback occurs on ANY inverse of that same target. Its inverse set is exactly the normalized B-fibre of the original y (one actual point here), and Q(z)=the suffix of y after tau0. If d wins on y, e(eta)=d(tau0 eta) is one total rational winning martingale on that suffix. This contradicts z in R2. QED.

The normalized source test can also be installed directly on the ORIGINAL input without raising cap: retain the first n0-1 input bits as an output tag and apply Q, preceded by the fixed first-bit flip, to the remaining input. The resulting globally fair map has cap TWO, because its retained tag identifies the deleted bits. It destroys x whenever the local Q does. This checks the same-source quantifier independently of a composition-of-two-cap-maps fallacy.

## C. Complete branching construction on noncomputable paths; restricted composition

Generalize S128's MAIN state to ACTIVE at an arbitrary decidable output tree. There are r tagged source sectors and N compatible cells, one sector doubled. At an ACTIVE node compute from its finite output prefix either HOLD, or SWITCH to a different tag with preferred next output bit b. At HOLD, send both children of the lexicographically first doubled source cell to output 0 and both of the other doubled cell to output 1; split every singleton by its fresh source bit. BOTH children remain ACTIVE with the same doubled tag. At SWITCH use S128's row: one child of the first old cell to b and the other to 1-b; both children of the second old cell to 1-b; both children of the new singleton to b; every remaining singleton splits. Preferred child stays ACTIVE with new doubled tag. Sibling is SAFE: the single old child is SOLO, the other two old children PAIR, and all remaining cells SOLO. SAFE uses S128's unchanged lexicographic pair reset and singleton splits forever.

All 2N source children are assigned exactly once; each output child has N cells. Therefore global fairness and total additive-c computation hold at every output. A path that ever leaves ACTIVE has N-2 SOLO inverses plus one PAIR inverse. A path staying ACTIVE has one actual inverse per initial tag: at every doubled-tag transition both HOLD and SWITCH reject one of the previous two cells on that particular output continuation, while singleton tags have at most one continuation candidate. Compactness gives existence in every nonempty tag and rejects a second path. Hence EXACT r=N-1 full inverse streams above EVERY output, including never-switching boundaries and every sibling. This permits uncountably many ACTIVE paths, rather than hardcoding an uncomputable spine.

Two separate clocks: (I) at even output depths SWITCH with b=0 and new=(old+1) mod r, and at odd depths HOLD; (II) at depths divisible by three SWITCH, with b equal to the last output bit (0 at the root) and new=(old+1) mod r, at other depths HOLD. Choosing the free HOLD bits to code any noncomputable alpha gives a noncomputable ACTIVE y and perpetual surplus circulation for each schedule. A total rational OUTPUT account doubles at each correctly followed SWITCH, holding at other nodes and stopping after a SAFE exit. All such selected y are nonCR. With these regularly spaced clocks their forced-bit test is also effectively null with computable rate, so they are NOT advertised as Schnorr random.

For arbitrary total computable ACTIVE-node schedules, infinite switching is excluded for every CR source by A129-1. After a finite switch sequence, SAFE paths or forever-HOLD paths have an output tail equal to the source tail, with a fixed finite offset (one delayed bit in the doubled/PAIR branch). Each output is obtained by finite coordinate deletion and finite flips from its source. S113 gives CR and every R_j preservation. Thus branching removes the computable-spine limitation but cannot supply three CR inverses over a bad output. Whether a Schnorr-random infinite-switch output exists for a suitably irregular clock is not proved or needed for the obstruction.

Composition: retain S128's exact-three necessity for G=H composed F with H,F in F2: H everywhere exact-two, and precisely one H inverse lies in F's double locus. No ramification/topological invariant forbidding an actual F3 factorization was established. A useful additional RESTRICTED consequence: if both factors have additive use one, the outer H is saturated (two cells/two true paths) and conserves all CR by S109. Every R2 source has CR F-image, hence CR G-image. Thus an actual R2-destructive exact-three map cannot have this two-one-lookahead factorization. This is a conditional obstruction, not an exhibited nonfactorable observer. T3 remains a valid factorable example. General F2(R2) closure, R2/R4 and arbitrary F3 factorization remain unresolved.

## D. Pointwise-finite observers: a local uniform-cap theorem

**D129-1 (local-cap reduction).** Let G be ANY total computable globally fair map, possibly with unbounded or infinite fibres elsewhere. Suppose x is in R_k, y=G(x), and some output cylinder V=[tau] containing y has at most k actual inverses over EVERY output in V. Then y is CR. No global cap for G is required.

Proof. C=G^-1(V) is computably clopen with lambda(C)=2^(-|tau|). Compute a common source length n>=|tau| for C; it consists of exactly 2^(n-|tau|) source n-cells. Enumerate these cells s_a in order, with index a a binary word of length l=n-|tau|. The computable homeomorphism J(a u)=s_a u maps normalized fair X to normalized lambda on C. Define Q(z) by deleting tau from G(J(z)). Its output distribution is fair by conditional global fairness on V. It is total computable and each full fibre has cardinal <=k by J's bijection. Thus Q is in F_k.

For x=s_a u, the normalized source a u belongs to R_k: delete the first |tau| bits of x, leaving a length-l prefix followed by u, then apply a fixed finite flip to that prefix to make it a. This uses finite deletion and finite flips only. Q(a u) is the suffix of y, hence CR; fixed-prefix CR equivalence gives y CR. If l=0 the same deletion gives u; all endpoint cases are covered. QED.

Define L_G={y: EVERY output neighborhood has UNBOUNDED actual fibre sizes}. Its complement is the union of locally capped cylinders, so L_G is closed; no effective closedness or measure claim is made. Any G destroying an R_fin source must send it into L_G. In S128's pointwise-finite-unbounded U, L_U={0^omega}, whose sole inverse is computable, independently explaining why it cannot separate the randomness classes. For a general pointwise-finite observer L_G can include outputs whose OWN fibre is finite: local boundedness must not be inferred from pointwise finiteness. No theorem that all such maps preserve R_fin, no measure-zero theorem for L_G, and no R_fin/pointwise-finite separation follows.
