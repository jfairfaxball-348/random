## 2. Front B/E — a substantial REGULAR-STRATA double-fibre preservation theorem

Definitions. A **finite-state synchronous transducer with delay c** is a fixed finite deterministic control set, a computable initializer reading the first c source bits, and a deterministic transition which for every subsequent input bit emits exactly one output bit and updates the control. Thus the first m output bits are read from the first m+c original input bits. We require every input to produce an infinite output; c and the machine are fixed, not functions of the particular source. Its map G is assumed FAIR and in F2 when the randomness conclusion is stated. No decision procedure for checking those two promises is asserted.

Recall S106's closed double strata D_r(G)={y: two ACTUAL inverse sources have distinct first r input bits}, r>=1. These are increasing and their union is the double-output locus. We prove a stronger property than effective closedness.

**Theorem B114-1 (effective finite-state safety presentation; internally proved).** For EVERY such finite-state transducer G and every r, a deterministic finite safety automaton A_r can be computed from its transition table, c and r, which reads y and rejects as soon as its state is EMPTY, and satisfies
    y in D_r(G) iff the A_r state is nonempty after EVERY output prefix.
In particular D_r is an effectively closed regular safety omega-language, and lambda(D_r) is a computable rational uniformly obtainable from the finite automaton. The statement of regularity does not need fairness or the F2 bound.

Proof, tracking ACTUAL paired histories. Initialise the finite set of ALL pairs of source c-bit prefixes and corresponding transducer control states; retain each pair's equality/difference flag over source positions <r and a counter up to r. After reading output symbol b, nondeterministically append one input bit to EACH of the two candidate runs, keep precisely those pairs whose TWO emitted symbols both equal b, and update both control states and the difference flag. At the first stage when at least r source bits have been consumed, permanently discard pairs with equal first-r source prefixes. Thereafter only the pair of finite control states is needed: even if two distinct histories arrive in the SAME control state, the distinct early prefixes remain witnessed and their identical subsequent bit continuation still produces TWO distinct sources. Take the deterministic powerset of all surviving paired states, with the initial r/c counter phase folded into finitely many states. Every output word tau has a surviving state iff there exist TWO finite input histories of length |tau|+c matching tau and unequal in their first r bits, for sufficiently long tau. The collection of such paired histories is a finitely branching tree closed under truncation after the r stage. Existence for EVERY extension tau=y|n is therefore, by König's lemma, equivalent to TWO ACTUAL infinite sources x0,x1 with G(xi)=y and x0|r != x1|r. The converse is immediate by taking true prefixes. This handles arbitrary infinite y, ghost pairs that die later, and identical transducer states with different pasts: no finite row is promoted prematurely to an actual inverse.

For a deterministic finite safety automaton with dead absorbing state, its fair-coin random-input process is a finite Markov chain with rational 1/2 transition probabilities. Probability of avoiding dead forever is computable rational: decompose its finite graph into closed recurrent communicating classes; classify all-dead and all-safe recurrent classes, then solve the finite rational linear system for absorption probabilities on transient states. Thus lambda(D_r) rational uniformly. No oracle for a noncomputable canonical D_r of an ARBITRARY G is obtained. QED.

**Theorem B114-2 (effective null-boundary elimination; internally proved).** If C is ANY effectively presented finite deterministic safety omega-language, then the topological boundary C\int(C) is EFFECTIVELY CLOSED of fair measure ZERO, with a uniformly computable geometric tail estimate derived from the finite automaton.

Proof. A reachable nondead automaton state is permanently safe precisely when NO finite word from it reaches dead. This is decidable by finite graph reachability. A word's cylinder is wholly in C iff its reached state is permanently safe. Hence int(C) is the c.e. union of those cylinders. The boundary consists of infinite words whose run reaches NEITHER dead NOR permanently safe, a computable-tree path set, hence effectively closed. Let M be the number of automaton states (take N=max(1,M)). Every remaining undecided state has some simple path to dead of length <=N. Conditioned on being in such a state, a fresh fair N-bit block follows one such path with probability >=2^(-N); such a path cannot pass through a permanently safe state on the way to dead. Therefore
    lambda{run undecided after kN steps} <= (1-2^(-N))^k.
The boundary is contained in every such event and has measure zero, with the written computable rate. Its paths may EXIST (infinite undecided paths), but none can be CR output of a CR source of a FAIR total computable map, as proved next. No assertion that boundary is empty is made. QED.

**Theorem B114-3 (regular-strata DP2; internally proved).** Let G be ANY total computable FAIR map in F2 such that each D_r(G) has a supplied effective finite safety-automaton presentation (not necessarily uniformly in r). Then EVERY computably random x whose y=G(x) has two ACTUAL preimages has computably random y. Consequently every fair F2 finite-state synchronous transducer preserves CR at EVERY actual double source, and every EVERYWHERE exactly-two map in that class preserves ALL CR (restricted CP2). Moreover no CR double-source image lies on any boundary(D_r) at a separating r.

Proof, independent route I. Choose r separating the actual two inverse sources. Then y in D_r. B114-2 gives a null effectively closed boundary B_r=D_r\int(D_r). If y belonged to B_r, then x belonged to G^-1(B_r), effectively closed by total computability and of fair measure lambda(B_r)=0 by G's fairness. A CR sequence is Kurtz random and cannot lie in an effectively closed null set; contradiction. Hence y belongs to int(D_r). S106's locally clopen two-sheet criterion applies at every CR inverse above such a y, and yields y CR. This uses NO computable measure name for D_r, NO positive conditional sheet limit and NO stopping-price oracle.

Proof, independent route II. By B114-1 (or the assumed finite presentation) the measure lambda(D_r) is computable rational. Apply S112's B112-2 with C=D_r, including zero sheet handling, to get the same CR conclusion. Route II uses the scoped S112 proof-inspected Rute Lemmas15-16; Route I does not. Neither is a proof of unrestricted DP2 because arbitrary computable G can have nonregular, noncomputable-measure D_r. QED.

**Corollary B114-4 (finite-state ramification obstruction).** In an everywhere exact-two fair finite-state transducer, every CR source has a continuous canonical partner at its actual image, since an interior D_r admits two continuous inverse charts. Thus no FINITE-STATE fair exact-two map yields a ramified CR double-source destroyer. ## 4. Front C — unsaturated four-state transitions, infinite fibre test and nonregular escape

For c=2, fair length-m output cylinders have exactly FOUR length-(m+2) preimage prefixes; in each transition each of their four rows has 0,1 or2 children sent to output symbol 0. Let n_i count rows with i zero-children. Balancing the eight children into four of each symbol gives n_0=n_2 and n_0+n_1+n_2=4. Thus the 70 row assignments are 16 fully balanced, 48 genuine hybrids with (n_0,n_1,n_2)=(1,2,1), and 6 full folds (2,0,2), accounting for the orientation choices. This independently recovers S109/S110's local 48-type frontier; these counts are FINITE candidates, NOT four actual inverse paths. If a finite-state machine generates arbitrarily many recurrent hybrid rows, B114-3 still rules out a bad CR ACTUAL DOUBLE image. This goes beyond claiming that folds eventually cease.

For ANY total computable fair G with computable use u(m) and u(m)>=m, there are exactly 2^(u(m)-m) source words of length u(m) mapping into any target m-cylinder. If u(m)-m is bounded by c, compactness gives <=2^c ACTUAL infinite inverses over every output. Hence an actual infinite fibre forces UNBOUNDED additive use excess for EVERY valid modulus u. Conversely unbounded excess alone proves neither a third inverse nor destruction: a computable fair coordinate permutation can have arbitrarily large necessary lookahead while being a computable inverse homeomorphism with singleton fibres. Even/odd coordinate projection is fair and has continuum fibres but is NOT in F2. Countably infinite fibres are also not excluded in general by compactness, so never replace "infinitely many" with "continuum" without proof. The actual F2 construction task must control persistent histories, not merely increase candidate width or turn on infinitely many folds.

The regular-strata theorem does not classify variable-use maps, nonfinite-state additive-two maps, or the original arbitrary G in F2. In particular S108's decidable nonregular fat nowhere-dense K may have CR boundary points and noncomputable D1 measure, so replacing its tree by a finite automaton destroys precisely the dangerous source geometry.
