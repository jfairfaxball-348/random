Proof of Thm6.9 reconstructed: Choose computable rational p_s>1 with bounded cumulative q_s=∏_{i≤s}p_i; set r_s=(p_s−1)/2 and computably long finite consecutive blocks I_s of length ℓ_s=ℓ(1+r_s,3). Enumerate partial rational martingale programs d_e; let E be the NONCOMPUTABLE set of total nonnegative normalized martingales. For L_e=Σ_{i≤e}ℓ_i and c_e=r_e/2^{L_e}, define aggregate dbar_s=Σ_{e≤s,e∈E}c_ed_e. From built prefix σ call length-ℓ_s extension τ admissible if dbar_{s−1}(στ)≤(1+r_s)dbar_{s−1}(σ); stage 0 is unrestricted. Martingale averaging plus the length choice supplies at least THREE admissible τ. Choose a canonical pair τ0,τ1 differing at least TWO coordinates: any three distinct binary strings of equal length contain such a pair. Use τ0 for s∉E and τ1 for s∈E, defining a noncomputable but existent Y.

Equation (6.7) establishes for every s that dbar_s(Y↾L_s)≤q_s. Initially c_0d_0(Y↾L_0)≤r_0≤q_0. Inductively previous terms cost ≤(1+r_s)q_{s−1} and the newly included term costs ≤c_s2^{L_s}=r_s; since q_{s−1}≥1,
dbar_s(Y↾L_s)≤(1+r_s)q_{s−1}+r_s≤(1+2r_s)q_{s−1}=p_sq_{s−1}=q_s.
If any computable martingale wins on Y by unbounded limsup, Remark 2.25 converts it to one winning by unbounded liminf (savings). That total d_i has positive coefficient c_i within all dbar_s for s≥i, contradicting the uniformly bounded q_s at block endpoints. The savings conversion is essential: endpoint boundedness alone does not exclude wins between endpoints.

On input j∈I_s the oracle program M queries every coordinate of I_0∪⋯∪I_s EXCEPT j. Full earlier blocks and reconstructed E(0),...,E(s−1) determine the canonical admissible pair in each block. For current s, τ0 and τ1 differ at ≥2 coordinates, so removing j leaves an observed separating coordinate; choose the alternative matching the oracle and output its jth bit. On the ORIGINAL Y, this reconstructs the selected stages, halts, and equals Y(j). Query use is strictly below computable block endpoint L_s; choose computable cap U(j)>L_s. The target computation never asks j. On siblings reconstructed stages may be inconsistent or search may diverge; global use remains bounded and self-query forbidden. This is wtt-autoreducibility, NOT truth-table-autoreducibility.
