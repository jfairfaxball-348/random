## THE GAP — one-page executive answer
The only automatic inclusion is R₃⊆R₂. Equality asks for **every** legal three-fibre observer G, **every** x surviving **every** two-fibre observer, and **every** ordinary total rational output martingale d: d cannot win on G(x). Separation needs ONE pair (x,G), the SAME x surviving ALL F₂ and legal G destroying x's output. Neither exists as an established result. R₂ is strictly smaller than CR by S011/S101, so CR alone is never a valid source certificate. MLR⊆R₃ by standard preservation of ML randomness under total fair computable maps; the inclusion does not establish the reverse.

One-hundred-plus sessions have developed effective sheet/charts, posterior-weight limits, source martingale compilers, full fair cap-three constructions, and restrictions on additive-use factorizations. They have **not** supplied either the uniform same-source bridge or a universally F₂-resistant survivor exposed to an F₃ destroyer. The number of decisive obligations is **two alternative finishing packages**, not one established universal missing lemma: a universal E-bridge OR a mutually compatible S-witness (legality + nonCR output + universal R₂ survival on the same source). Each package can be completed through non-equivalent routes. No percentage of completion is justified.

## Scope and logical normal forms
Let X=2^ω, λ the fair product measure. F_k contains total computable λ-preserving functions G:X→X with |G⁻¹(y)|≤k for EVERY complete y, counting genuine infinite paths (not temporary source cells). R_k={x:∀G∈F_k G(x)∈CR}. RAT-MART means total computable nonnegative rational-valued d with d(σ0)+d(σ1)=2d(σ); succeeds iff sup_n d(y↾n)=∞. F₁⊆F₂⊆F₃, so R₃⊆R₂.

E: ∀x[(∀F∈F₂∀d∈RAT-MART d fails on F(x))⇒(∀G∈F₃∀d∈RAT-MART d fails on G(x))].
S: ∃x∃G∈F₃∃d∈RAT-MART[(∀F∈F₂∀e∈RAT-MART e fails on F(x)) ∧ d succeeds on G(x)].
These are exact negations given R₃⊆R₂. In particular no switch x=x_F, no null-exception waiver for G, and no computable-real witness left unrationalised.

## Major S139 audit finding: check the QUALITATIVE inverse theorem before demanding a rate
This is a **re-use implication requiring hostile S140 source/hypothesis review, NOT a newly announced theorem**. For ANY total computable T, given output prefix τ one can compute its clopen preimage and the finite source m-prefixes compatible with it. For an output y having a UNIQUE complete inverse, compactness implies: for every m, eventually only one compatible source m-prefix remains. Search for that stage; this is a *partial* inverse algorithm J(y). Its domain includes every singleton-fibre output and is a Π⁰₂ domain defined by ∀m∃n singleton compatible m-prefix. If singleton outputs have λ-measure one, J is a.e. computable; global fairness + a.e. invertibility should make (T,J) an a.e. measure-preserving isomorphism, **potentially invoking SRC-0015/THM-0038 and S102 without ANY computable convergence rate**.

Exact S140 verification gates: (i) nonempty compatible source cells on ALL target prefixes; (ii) the Π⁰₂ domain and consistent monotone answers; (iii) reciprocal a.e. identities and inverse pushforward fairness; (iv) exact Rute/S102 inverse-domain hypothesis; (v) inclusion of ALL computably random points in required domains. If all pass, *a.e.-singleton F₃ observers are qualitatively CR-conserving regardless of noncomputable ambiguity modulus*. S138's explicit compiler remains a self-contained effective alternative, not a necessary hypothesis. In particular a purported noncomputable-rate **a.e.-singleton** G cannot separate; distinguish non-effective RATE from failure of a.e. UNIQUENESS. This audit is a possible route **retirement**, not a proof offered as novel S139 mathematics.

This does not cover positive-measure double/triple locus, conditional multi-sheet charts, or an exceptional set which the effective-isomorphism source does not cover. Nor does it establish that all F₃ are a.e. singleton: the fair bit-deletion F₂ has degree TWO everywhere, and lies in F₃. Thus a statement 'every F₃ admits A_m(n)→0' is plainly FALSE. A generic F₃→CR conservation theorem is also FALSE because S011 gives a destructive F₂⊆F₃ on some CR x. Any valid universal E bridge must **use x∈R₂**, not CR alone.

## S138 precise ambiguity quantity and computational distinction
For total computable fair T and integers m,n, let C_m(τ)={s∈2^m:[s]∩T⁻¹[τ]≠∅}, an exactly decidable finite set. A_m(n)=2⁻ⁿ # {τ∈2^n:|C_m(τ)|≥2}. This is an exact rational, nonincreasing in n. Compactness identifies lim_n A_m(n) with λ{y: at least two distinct inverse points disagree within their first m bits}. If T is a.e singleton, A_m(n)→0 for every m, but this monotone computable sequence need not, *from its mere convergence*, possess a computable modulus. S138 obtains A_m(n)≤(255/256)^t for n≥8(ceil(m/8)+2+t), with μ_k≤2^(k+1)λ after stopping. These facts prove explicit rational source martingale transfer for one G; they should not be confused with an implication all F₃ have a computable modulus, or with an obstruction to E-ISO when no modulus is known.

A_min maps measure-weighted COMPATIBILITY, not actual finite cardinal fibre cap. Finite aligned cells can be 4 forever even when the actual inverse count is ONE. Rate may be a useful constructive compiler input without being a necessary qualitative random-conservation condition.
