**E-path E**: local E-ISO for conull singleton strata, E-09 on covered residual multivalued strata, E-11 direct F₂ tests on uncovered residual; requires TRUE global exhaustive cover, not a.e-only declaration.

### Three distinct strictness pathways
(A) Construct x by a genuine full R₂ theorem (not merely CR or all-scan), then construct a legal G,d bad on that same x. (B) Construct G,d with an effectively characterized bad-image preimage class, THEN prove that class meets R₂ with an explicit forcing/nonempty argument. (C) Construct x,G,d **together** via prioritized source/output tree and global fair completion, checking conflicts and legal code promises. All require S-01–04; S-06 etc route-specific, not necessary in abstract.

### Decisive S138 comparator
A_m(n)=2⁻ⁿ #{τ of length n: at least 2 compatible source m-prefixes}. Exact dyadic rational, monotone and convergent; a.e singleton ⇒ limit 0 but not automatically computably rapid. S138 gives specific uniform exponential decay, stopped μ_k dominated by 2^(k+1)λ, and an ordinary rational source compiler (not merely computable-real). However an E-ISO qualitative shortcut may make lack of modulus irrelevant for singleton-conull G. S140 **first** verify E-10 in exact source; DO NOT invest in constructing slow-rate singleton countermodels as alleged separator without failing E-10 at an identified axiom. For positive-measure multivalued G, A_m(n) need not tend to zero at all.
