## Executive judgement for the owner

The project has established a substantial mathematical boundary, not its proposed new randomness notion. Injective total fair observations preserve computable randomness. Allowing two inverse points can destroy it, even when the destroyed computably random source has a singleton fibre. Raw scans with any fixed finite number of permanently missing bits all define the same robustness class OH. OH contains non-Martin-Löf-random points, but general two-fibre observations can destroy some of those points: **R₂⊊OH**. Subsequent work proves survival for increasingly broad restricted families and defeats selected survivors with recoded self-readers. It does not produce a point surviving every F₂ map, or a destroyer for every CR∖MLR point.

The most productive advances were S001/S011, S032/S033, and S081–S090. Long runs of ticket, certificate-clock and hazard refinements yielded soundly scoped machinery but did not settle the missing same-source implication. S092–S099 sharpened the gap: orientation is autoreducibility in a computable fair frame; analytic all-map protection can select an MLR point; and effective null coding must pay for its choices without turning the construction into a Kurtz or Schnorr test. Another local obstacle is unlikely to resolve that gap by itself.

**Recommendation:** S101 should reconstruct a small, complete internal proof dossier for the established S081–S083 package, grounded in S001/S011/S032/S033. This is mathematical consolidation for possible later external checking, with no contact or manuscript now. Keep R₂ versus MLR as the flagship question, but remove it as the automatic next-session proof-search default. Fallback: one bounded test of a genuinely different, non-orientation family of width-two source partitions, conditional on an explicit coverage bridge. No automatic return to paid-branch refinements. Review after at most five separately invoked sessions; a theorem dossier without an unresolved mathematical premise is a useful outcome even if the flagship remains undecided. Novelty and publishability remain unverified.

### 5.1 S001–S012: cardinality, geometry and partiality

**S001.** Compact range plus fairness is onto: an omitted output has a positive-measure cylinder omitted. Distinct input n-cylinders have disjoint compact images. The computable forward-use modulus lets a finite output-prefix separation be searched; compactness proves termination. Thus the inverse is total computable and fair. **S003/S007.** If three n-cylinders remain compatible at arbitrarily deep output cells, a finitely branching bad-output tree has a path; nested compactness yields three distinct preimages, contradicting the ALL-output bound. Search over the finite clopen data finds c(n). Coherence follows by monotone resampling. At a genuine double fibre, after the two actual prefixes split there is no room for a phantom at that precision; singleton phantoms need not have a computable last disappearance time. Counting prefixes is not computing probabilities. S002/S005/S006 provide explicit selector, mass and hitting-effectivity counterexamples. None of those examples alone is a CR destroyer.

### 5.4 S081–S084: leading constructive chain

**Bounded-hole collapse.** The bad-transcript tree below each finite source precision compactifies to a computable frontier. Held intervals have clique number ≤h and admit online first-fit coloring into h colors. **S082 consistency potential.** For partial assignment σ and output-cell capital d(w), let c indicate whether the cell hits [σ], and s whether it hits both refinements at a new coordinate. Then c_0+c_1=c+s and

\[
\tfrac12(\Psi(\sigma_0,t)+\Psi(\sigma_1,t))=\Psi(\sigma,t)+\gamma(\sigma,k,t).
\]

Hit indicators decrease on cell refinement; fairness and nonnegative martingales give time monotonicity, and conditional expected capital is dominated by Ψ. The bounded-hole frontier, or one candidate per block for block-recoded scans, bounds the sum of split weights. A finite decidable minimization therefore halts on every true validity prefix. Each stage fixes 2i+2 fresh coordinates; at most 2^(i+1) guessed outputs each have measure 2^(−(i+1)(i+2)). The union is an ordinary uniformly c.e. ML test; wrong guesses may hang and need not finish. The true validity set may be noncomputable, but **the enumeration of all completed guesses is computable**. This distinction is what later paid-branch attempts must reproduce.

The savings wrapper has active capital <2 and a nondecreasing bank with expectation ≤1.
