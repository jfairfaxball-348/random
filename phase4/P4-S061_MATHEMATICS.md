## 3. One computable source martingale for ALL possible epoch starts

Let beta=15/16 and set, for each clopen E and raw prefix sigma,
\[
p_E(\sigma)=2^{|\sigma|}\lambda(E\cap[\sigma]).
\]
This is a uniformly computable rational nonnegative fair raw martingale in sigma. Define
\[
d_B(\sigma)=\sum_{m=0}^\infty\frac1{(m+1)^2}
\sum_{h\in H_m}\sum_{k=1}^\infty
\beta^k(8/7)^k\,p_{E^B_{h,k}}(\sigma).
\tag{*}
\]

**Lemma 2 (effective portfolio).** (*) is a total computable-real, nonnegative, exactly fair raw source martingale with a computable finite root value, and for every sigma the double series has a computable uniform tail.

**Proof.** By Lemma 1 and p_E(sigma)<=2^{|sigma|}lambda(E), for every m,k,
\[
\sum_{h\in H_m}\beta^k(8/7)^k p_{E^B_{h,k}}(\sigma)
\le 2^{|\sigma|}\,\#H_m\,2^{-m}\beta^k
\le 2^{|\sigma|}\beta^k.
\]
Thus d_B(sigma)<=2^{|sigma|}(sum_{m>=0}(m+1)^{-2})(sum_{k>=1}beta^k), with effective rational error bounds from the computable tail of sum 1/(m+1)^2 and geometric beta-tail. All finite summands are rational computable and exactly fair, so the effective limit is fair. The P4-S059 positive-root-buffer and sibling-difference rationalization yields a total computable NONNEGATIVE rational fair martingale D_B, with |D_B(sigma)-(d_B(sigma)+c)| bounded uniformly by <1 for some fixed positive computable rational root offset c, and therefore D_B succeeds whenever d_B does. No noncomputable optional projection or limit of an unbounded open event is taken. QED.

**Theorem 3 (new actual-source LOCAL logarithmic overrun-spacing law; CONDITIONAL).** Define the total computable integer
\[
K(m)=64\lceil\log_2(m+2)\rceil\quad(m\ge0).
\]
Fix ANY total computable L and ANY total computable prospective epoch-start B. On the committed computably random X, either T_L has only finitely many actually profitable old turnovers, OR, if it has infinitely many, then
\[
\boxed{\ \exists E\ \forall e\ge E\ \exists j\in[e,e+K(m_e)):\quad W_j(X)\ge B(j,p_j).\ }
\tag{LS}
\]
Equivalently: beyond finitely many epoch starts, there is NO run of K(m_e) consecutive completed profitable epochs starting at e whose INDIVIDUAL completed miss counts are all below their own prospective computable budgets. This holds separately for EACH total computable B; E need not be computable from B or X.

**Proof.** Assume infinite actual profit and suppose contrary to (LS) that infinitely many e have W_j<B(j,p_j) for every j in [e,e+K(m_e)). Let h_e be the actual output word at epoch start e. By Lemma 1, X lies in the computably clopen E^B_{h_e,K(m_e)}. Consequently, at some sufficiently long raw prefix of X, its p_E equals 1, and the corresponding summand in (*) alone yields
\[
d_B(X{\upharpoonright}r)\ \ge\
\frac{(15/14)^{K(m_e)}}{(m_e+1)^2}.
\]
Since (1+1/14)^{16}>1+16/14+120/196>2, we have (15/14)^{64}>16. Therefore
\[
(15/14)^{K(m_e)}>(m_e+2)^4,
\quad
d_B(X{\upharpoonright}r)>(m_e+2)^4/(m_e+1)^2.
\]
Infinite actual profitable epochs force m_e to tend to infinity. Infinitely many contrary epochs therefore make the SINGLE fixed raw computable rational D_B unbounded on X, contradicting X in CR. QED.
