## B. Exact two-candidate ghost dynamics and capital/stopping obstruction

Assume G has additive use TWO, y has two actual inverses x_0,x_1 separated by first-bit clopen cones, and the S117 stable-half hypothesis holds for B=[0]. After finitely many output levels, each of B and its complement has exactly TWO source (m+2)-candidates over y|m. Their four child rows split 2:2 WITHIN each cone along BOTH next output children, because the actual-half martingale value forces sibling half. For each cone at each such output prefix, its two rows are either:
- **PERM/PERM:** each row has one child emitting 0 and one emitting 1; or
- **FOLD:** one row emits 00 (two 0 children), the other emits 11 (two 1 children).
This follows by exhausting integer contributions (0,1,2) summing to two; no intermediate 1:3 split can be silently inserted.

**B118-1 (INFINITE RESET GHOST THEOREM).** In each cone where y has exactly ONE ACTUAL infinite inverse x and exactly TWO finite compatible candidates at every sufficiently late output depth, the FOLD transition occurs INFINITELY often along y. At any stage PERM/PERM, the candidate distinct from x has exactly one child continuing along y. At a FOLD the actual candidate must be the row that emits the actual next output bit twice, while the other row has NO compatible child: the old ghost dies and a new candidate sibling of x is born. If only finitely many folds occurred, from the last fold onward the other candidate would have a unique descendant over EVERY remaining y prefix, yielding a SECOND actual inverse by compactness, contradiction. Moreover for EVERY fixed source prefix length r, both late candidates in that cone extend x|r: the finitely many other r-cones each eventually become empty of compatible candidates, otherwise compactness gives a different actual inverse. QED.

The folds in the TWO cones need NOT be simultaneous: locally, the pair of side transitions may be PERM/PERM in both, FOLD in one and PERM/PERM in the other, or FOLD in both. The separately required two infinite fold sets are not a common effective source-capital schedule. At a FOLD the winning source row outputs the SAME bit on both input children, so the S109/S117 gated source compiler freezes precisely when an output martingale might grow. No finite-population lower bound on its own repairs that missing capital growth. This is a useful synchronization specification, not a universal impossibility theorem. In particular an FOLD at every node can merely realize an identity homeomorphism in a one-bit-delayed chart; infinitely many folds alone do not destroy CR.

**B118-2 (Schnorr-strength ceiling of naive stopping pullback).** For ANY total computable fair G (not just F2), G sends every CR source to a Schnorr-random output. For a uniform Schnorr test (U_n), effectively open and with uniformly computable lambda(U_n)<=2^-n, its preimages G^-1(U_n) are effectively open, have the SAME uniformly computable measures by fairness, and form a Schnorr test. Applying this to arbitrary output martingale hitting sets U_n={y: exists m, d(y|m)>2^n} does NOT transfer CR: their measures are bounded by 2^-n but need not be uniformly computable, and an arbitrary ML-test is not a Schnorr test. Attempts to mix the individual source accounts from both half-price charts fail at the precise FOLD row: capital factors p(tau b)/p(tau) do not average to one when both children emit the same b.
