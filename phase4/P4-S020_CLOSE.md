The P4-S019 halting-coded construction can be heartbeatized. Insert summably small scaled deterministic-trigger tickets through the control/machine-simulation region, and terminate positive tickets after an unfavourable heartbeat. Then every branch which will ever realize more positive skipped gain realizes some positive gain again within a fixed computable number of epochs. The heartbeat total is bounded, so divergent machine branches still have finite E, while a halt after t steps still unlocks an effectively divergent finite burst. Every b(K) remains finite, yet a computable majorant would again bound halting times and decide the halting problem.

Thus the obstruction is **loss-scale nonuniformity**, not merely long stretches with E exactly constant.

Finite search then decides Reach(K,m), equivalently Bar(K,m).
