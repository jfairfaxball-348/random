## B170.1: finite complete fibres imply relative inversion with finite advice

**Theorem (scoped, internally proved).** Let G:2^omega->2^omega be ANY TOTAL computable Cantor map, with NO fairness assumption. Fix y for which the COMPLETE inverse fibre G^-1{y} is FINITE. For EACH x in this fibre, a finite binary word s (a prefix of x separating it from all other full inverses of y) can be hardcoded into one partial oracle functional Phi_s such that Phi_s^y is TOTAL and equals x. Since y=G(x) <=_T x, we have x ≡_T y for EACH such x, NONUNIFORMLY in its isolating branch word. The conclusion remains true for a pointwise-finite map with no uniform fibre cap; do NOT strengthen it to a uniform computable inverse selector or computable sheet prices.

**Proof.** Since the complete fibre above y is finite, choose a prefix s of x incompatible with every other member (if singleton, s can be empty). Then K=[s] intersects G^-1{y} in precisely {x}. For each coordinate n, define compact clopen K_b={z∈[s]: z(n)=b}, b=0,1. Because G is TOTAL computable on Cantor space, G(K_b) is an *effectively compact* subset, uniformly from s,n,b and the given total map index: finite-output covers can be exhaustively certified by simulating the computation on all source cylinders using compactness. The predicate y NOT IN G(K_b) is therefore semidecidable relative to oracle y: search a finite output prefix [y|m] disjoint from the compact image G(K_b), certifiable from a finite cover. Exactly one of K_0,K_1 contains x, hence for the opposite bit y∉G(K_b); that search eventually halts. Run both searches and return the bit opposite the first certified exclusion. This computes x(n) from y (if n<|s| the answer also follows from s). Termination is promised only on the fixed target y and isolating branch s; no uniform effective method picks s from y. Forward y<=_T x follows from total computability of G. QED (INTERNAL).

**H1 consequence and limitation.** Every legal F_k observer has finite fibres, so a putative nonCR output y=G(x) on a CR source x necessarily satisfies y≡_T x. ## C170.1: fair a.e. computable isomorphism with NO total continuous extension even on CR inputs

Let D=2^omega\\{0^omega}. Every x∈D admits exactly one expression x=0^n 1 b z, n∈N, b∈{0,1}, z∈2^omega. Define
    P(0^n 1 b z) = b 0^n 1 z.
Let E=2^omega\\{0^omega,10^omega}. Every y∈E has the unique form y=b 0^n 1 z, and its inverse is P^-1(b0^n1z)=0^n1bz.

**Theorem.** (i) P:D->E and P^-1:E->D are partial computable mutually inverse maps, total on their stated conull domains, hence an a.e. computable isomorphism. (ii) They preserve fair measure: for all Borel B in E, lambda(P^-1(B))=lambda(B), with the two complementary finite exceptional sets null. (iii) There is NO TOTAL continuous map H:2^omega->2^omega that agrees with P on ALL computably random source inputs, hence no total computable extension agreeing on all D. (iv) P preserves computable randomness on EVERY CR source; it is NOT an F_k detector or separation witness.

**Proof (i).** Search the input stream until the first 1, then read the succeeding bit b, emit b followed by the observed n zeros, 1, and the untouched tail z. All output bits can be emitted after finite input use, for each x∈D. The inverse searches for the first 1 after the output's initial bit b. Both domain and range are effective G_delta measure-one domains (indeed complements of finite closed singletons).

**Proof (ii).** The disjoint cylinders [0^n 1 b] partition D, and [b 0^n 1] partition E. On each corresponding cylinder P moves the prefix and leaves z unchanged. Both cylinders have identical measure 2^{-(n+2)}, and relative tail measure is unchanged. Therefore a measure-preserving bijection holds componentwise; countable additivity yields global Borel preservation. These are EXACT cylinder probabilities, not a mere a.e.-fibre count.

**Proof (iii).** Fix ANY computably random z. Finite prefixing preserves CR, so a_n=0^n 1 0 z and c_n=0^n 1 1 z are CR for every n. Both converge to 0^omega. Yet P(a_n)=0 0^n 1 z tends to 0^omega while P(c_n)=1 0^n 1 z tends to 10^omega; those limits differ. If a total continuous H agreed with P on all CR sources, continuity at 0^omega would force H(0^omega) to equal two distinct outputs. Contradiction. Note: these CR paths are used to defeat a proposed EXTENSION of P; they are not SAME-SOURCE H1 counterexamples.

**Proof (iv).** If x=0^n 1 b z is CR, its tail z is CR by invariance under removing a fixed finite prefix. The image b0^n1z is CR by the reverse finite-prefix invariance. Thus P is a CR-preserving a.e. fair isomorphism, not a destroyer. QED (INTERNAL).
