## Countermeasure tests for every route
- **Exact input-output quantifiers:** does the lemma take ∀G or one G? Is its conclusion about the SAME x, not a freshly selected x? Does it quantify ∀d or select ONE winning d?
- **Totality:** partial inverse/oracle cannot silently become total observer; degree≤3 must hold for every complete y, not almost every y or finite rows.
- **Fairness:** check λG⁻¹[τ]=2⁻|τ| for every finite τ, with sibling conservation; do not infer from one output spine.
- **Effectivity:** state whether produced probabilities are rational, computable REAL, lower semicomputable, or noncomputable; spell out a tail error bound before calling a source martingale total rational.
- **Separation:** one candidate x must avoid S011/S083/S101 and ALL other legal F₂ maps, not just listed destroyers.
- **Proof-source:** direct original theorem hypotheses and printed proof, not a 2016 citation of an earlier assertion.
