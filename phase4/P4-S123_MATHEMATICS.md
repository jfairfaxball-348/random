## 1. A fresh savings lemma with a persistent subtree lower bound

Let d be ANY total nonnegative rational martingale, normalized to d(empty)=1 (zero-root case trivial). For k>=1 stop d at its first prefix where d>=2^k, and call the resulting frozen ordinary rational martingale d_k. Define

    S(s) = sum_(k>=1) 2^(-k) d_k(s).

This is an **exactly computable RATIONAL**, TOTAL martingale. For a fixed s let M(s)=max_{rho prefix s} d(rho). Whenever 2^k>M(s), d_k(s)=d(s). So the infinite geometric tail collapses to (sum_(k>K)2^(-k))d(s); only finitely many k need simulation. Each d_k obeys the fair-child identity even at a first-hit node, and sum weights=1.

If d wins on y, then for every N there is a finite y-prefix sigma at which thresholds 2^1,...,2^N have all been hit. Their stopped capitals are thereafter >=2^k on **EVERY extension** of sigma. Therefore for every finite extension z of sigma, S(z)>=N. In particular S(y|n) tends to infinity, and the stronger *uniform subtree persistence* holds. This is stronger than merely unbounded limsup and is the key needed for non-cylindrical clopen images.

S is nonnegative and coherent, hence it defines a computable finite Borel measure nu by nu([tau])=2^(-|tau|) S(tau). Every clopen nu-mass is an EXACT computable rational. We do NOT need to calculate any infinite stopping probability or posterior last-change time.

## 2. Rational capital through ANY effective fair homeomorphism

    D_H(s) = 2^|s| nu(H([s])).

It is total, exactly rational and nonnegative. Since H([s0]) and H([s1]) partition H([s]), D_H(s0)+D_H(s1)=2D_H(s) and D_H(empty)=1. ## 4. Theorem B123: the exact inverse SET distinction is sharp for G_U

This distinction blocks any incorrect inference from 'no selector' to 'no finite candidate set'.

**B123-2 (INTERNALLY PROVED, randomized half ceiling).** Suppose a probabilistic oracle algorithm uses fair independent coins, is ALMOST SURELY TOTAL (outputs an infinite candidate stream for probability-one of coins) on EVERY CR output, and guarantees a genuine inverse with probability at least 1/2+delta for some FIXED delta>0 on EVERY such output. This is impossible. At a CR y in K, its first output source bit is decided on some finite output prefix rho for a coin set of probability >1-epsilon (by almost-sure totality and monotone convergence of finite-prefix first-bit computation probabilities). Among those early-decided coins, choose the LESS likely key c, with mass <=1/2. Density of U gives a first-trigger cylinder extending rho; choose the released output bit c and then a CR output y' in that positive-measure cylinder. Every genuine inverse of y' starts with c, while all early-decided wrong-key coin runs are unchanged from their computations at y. Hence success probability at y' is <=1/2+epsilon. For epsilon<delta contradiction. Conversely pick a fair random initial key a, output C_a(y): it is correct with probability 1 on K and 1/2 on triggered outputs. So 1/2 is the OPTIMAL UNIFORM lower bound for this model. The result applies to almost-surely total randomized selectors with a uniform-in-y advantage.
