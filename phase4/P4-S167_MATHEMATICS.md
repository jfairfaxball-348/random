## A167 — PRIMARY H1 proof attempt, positive direction

Definitions: `F_k` consists of total computable, globally fair maps G:2^omega->2^omega with at most k ACTUAL COMPLETE inverse streams over EVERY output. `R_fin=intersection_k R_k`. H1 asks whether every x in CR but not MLR has some one finite k and one G in F_k with G(x) nonCR. One finite k may depend on x but NOT the output of that chosen G.

Fix an arbitrary nonMLR CR x and an effective decreasing ML test (U_n) capturing x. An attempted 'test-exposure' transformation detects successive entered test cylinders, predicts bits the source will reveal, and emits a fair adaptive scan. The key unproved step is that x's repeated membership in open U_n yields arbitrarily many **fresh, pre-revelation source bits** predicted correctly by a partial effective machine that may diverge on other sources. Open-test membership supplies a finite certificate only *after* its queried bits have been read; it does NOT supply pre-revelation predictions. If such predictions were instead globally computably total on all source prefixes before each fresh bit, the exactly rational capital-two-on-each-prediction source martingale would succeed on x, contradicting x in CR. Thus total predictions cannot substitute for S011's branchwise-partial autoreduction. S011 works for a specially supplied CR wtt-autoreducible Y; no theorem states EVERY nonMLR CR x has the needed self-avoiding partial prediction structure. Consequently this positive attempt does NOT prove H1.

A second attempt composes `F_{k_i}` maps to expose successive distinct ML deficiencies. Every FINITE chain has global cap `product_i k_i` by S127, with exact preservation of fairness/totality. No finite chain is proved sufficient for arbitrary x; an INFINITE chain need not be a total computable observer at all, much less have a finite global cap, since products may diverge and a limiting map requires an independent totality/fibre proof. Selecting different finite chains for different output prefixes does not give ONE G. This avenue remains OPEN rather than a theorem.

## B167 — PRIMARY H1 counterexample attempt, negative direction

A negative answer needs ONE source `x in CR\MLR` such that for EVERY finite k, every G in F_k and every total rational output martingale d, `sup_n d(G(x)|n)<infinity`. Writing x_k for each k, or building CR sources without controlling nonMLR, is insufficient. At fixed valid (G,d), because G is fair, its d-success set pulls back to a lambda-null source set; the countable union over all valid programs remains null. Thus R_fin has full lambda-measure (indeed MLR is already full measure). **This does not imply** R_fin intersects CR\MLR: the latter is lambda-null, and a conull set can be disjoint from it. There is also no effective decidable list of exactly the valid total fair finite-cap programs, nor any guarantee that a common survivor selected measure-theoretically fails a universal ML test. No single nonMLR survivor is built. Negative H1 remains OPEN.

## C167 — New internal general-j multiplicity support lemma (structural fallback, NOT H1 closure)

**Theorem C167.1 (uniform effective F_sigma multiplicity).** For ANY total computable Cantor map G, with NO fairness or fibre bound assumed, and each integer j>=2, the set
`E_j(G)={y: there are >=j pairwise distinct COMPLETE streams x with G(x)=y}`
is uniformly effectively Sigma^0_2 (effective F_sigma).

**Proof.** For each n define C_{j,n} as the finite union over ordered j-tuples of pairwise distinct input n-words s_1,...,s_j of `intersection_{i=1}^j G([s_i])`. Every input cylinder [s] is effectively compact. A total computable function on the effectively compact Cantor space has a computable output-use modulus: for each output precision m, search a finite full source-prefix cover on which the first m output bits are all determined. Thus its image G([s]) is effectively compact and, in Cantor space, uniformly effectively closed. Finite intersections/unions make C_{j,n} uniformly effectively closed. If y belongs to C_{j,n}, there exist j distinct complete inverse streams with distinct n-prefixes. Conversely j distinct streams are pairwise separated at a common sufficiently large n, hence y lies in C_{j,n}. Therefore E_j=union_n C_{j,n}, uniformly effective F_sigma. No finite-level source-cell count is confused with full-fibre degree. QED (internal).

**Theorem C167.2 (Schnorr-random positive-mass multiplicity support).** If y is Schnorr random and y has at least j complete G-inverses, then at some finite separation n, y lies in C_{j,n} with **strictly positive** fair measure. Indeed for EVERY n with y in C_{j,n}, lambda(C_{j,n})>0. In particular, if lambda(E_j(G))=0, then NO Schnorr-random y belongs to E_j(G).

**Proof.** A uniformly effectively closed null class C has computable descending clopen covers with measures tending to zero. For each r search for a finite clopen cover V_r of C of dyadic measure <=2^(-r-2); because lambda(C)=0 every search terminates. The uniformly computable list (V_r) has finite computably summable total measure with an effective geometric tail and hence is a Schnorr-Solovay test; every member of C belongs to infinitely many V_r, so no Schnorr-random point lies in C. Apply this to C_{j,n} if it is null. If E_j is null, every C_{j,n} is null, so no Schnorr-random point lies in their union. QED (internal). The need for a uniform test over *all* n can alternatively be met by cover V_{n,r} of each null C_{j,n} with measure <=2^(-n-r-3), with geometric effective tails.

**Corollary C167.3 (a.e. bound on random output fibres, NOT F_k legality).** For ANY total computable globally fair G such that `lambda({y:|G^-1(y)|>k})=0`, and ANY x in CR, the COMPLETE fibre of G(x) has at most k streams. Proof: fair total computable G sends Schnorr-random x (including every CR x) to Schnorr-random y by pulling back all Schnorr tests with unchanged computable measures. Apply C167.2 with j=k+1. Note the map G can still have outputs with >k inverses at NONrandom y, so this result does NOT put G in F_k and does NOT detect an arbitrary nonMLR CR source. QED (internal).

**Effective-pricing obstruction C167.4.** Positivity of lambda(C_{j,n}) is purely existential: one cannot compute a uniform positive rational lower bound for arbitrary positive effectively closed classes. Example: for a Turing machine e, let a decreasing computable clopen approximation stay at the whole space until e halts, then become empty. Its effective closed limit is whole space when e never halts and empty when e halts. Deciding positive measure uniformly for these classes would decide nonhalting. Thus C167.2 does NOT yield an effective sheet-selection weight or winning rational source martingale; the central H1 finance/selection step is absent. This obstruction addresses the proposed blanket 'positive compact support => computably priced selector' inference, not every possible compiler.

This generalises S006's effective F_sigma fact for the DOUBLE-fibre locus, and should NOT be labelled a literature-new theorem without prior-art checking. The strength here is uniform arbitrary j and a Schnorr-random support consequence, not a resolved randomness-class inclusion.

## D167 — H4 benchmark checked, not promoted as new or as a separator

Let P(1^n 0 b t)=1^n 0 t with b any n-bit word, and P(1^omega)=1^omega. All outputs first having zero after n ones have EXACTLY 2^n complete sources; the boundary 1^omega has EXACTLY ONE complete source. For output prefix length m, source prefix length 2m determines the output prefix; at the boundary the leading ones agree, so P is everywhere total computable and continuous. On each [1^n0], deleting n fair bits preserves fair distribution conditional on that cylinder, while the excluded singleton has zero mass; hence lambda(P^-1[tau])=2^(-|tau|) for EVERY output cylinder. Therefore P lies in pointwise-finite fair observers but in NO global F_k. On a CR source x, the first zero occurs at finite n (the all-ones boundary is computable nonCR); deleting a fixed finite block preserves CR (S113). Thus P preserves every CR source and is a NON-SEPARATOR: family inclusion is strict, randomness-class inclusion not shown strict. Exact prefix checks m=1..7 are FINITE sanity only.
