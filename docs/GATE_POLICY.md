# Gate Policy

## General rule

A phase transition is a research claim about readiness. It requires evidence, not momentum.

For every gate:
1. complete the phase deliverables;
2. audit them against the exit criteria;
3. enumerate unresolved weaknesses;
4. record PASS, FAIL or BACKTRACK;
5. update authoritative state;
6. only then begin the next phase.

## Blockers

A blocker is anything that prevents an honest gate decision or authorized next action. Examples include inaccessible primary sources central to a priority claim, contradictory definitions, unresolved provenance, owner decisions, needed external communication, failed proof dependencies or venue-policy uncertainty.

Do not work around a gate blocker by silently weakening the standard.

## Gate 1 — Research/Catalogue -> Discovery

Minimum evidence:
- documented coverage plan and completed coverage audit;
- searchable source catalogue using stable source IDs;
- definition/terminology index;
- theorem/characterization index;
- implication/equivalence/separation relationship map;
- open-question/status records with source provenance;
- author/topic indexes;
- search/retrieval log;
- explicit source-access gaps and uncertainty register;
- no consequential unsourced claims in the authoritative research summary.

PASS means the library is fit to support discovery. It does not mean literally all literature has been found.

## Gate 2 — Discovery -> Novelty/Prior Art

Minimum evidence:
- bounded candidate portfolio;
- each candidate stated precisely enough to search for prior art;
- motivation and potential mathematical value;
- relationship to known concepts;
- plausible theorem package or characterization target;
- falsifiers/failure conditions;
- dependencies and expected difficulty;
- explicit separation between "appears interesting" and "is novel".

## Gate 3 — Novelty/Prior Art -> Mathematics

Minimum evidence for the selected candidate:
- dedicated primary-source prior-art audit;
- alternate terminology and equivalent formulations searched;
- closest known work identified;
- equivalence/rebranding risk addressed;
- significance/usefulness case;
- likely interested research communities;
- remaining novelty uncertainty explicit;
- documented selection decision or NO-GO.

If no candidate survives, backtrack to Phase 2.

## Gate 4 — Mathematics -> Publication

Minimum evidence:
- exact definition and claim set frozen;
- proofs checked and dependencies explicit;
- counterexamples/boundaries documented;
- computational evidence clearly separated from proof;
- formalisation/Palomar status recorded if used;
- final prior-art refresh performed;
- manuscript-worthy coherent contribution exists;
- no known fatal claim gap.

## Gate 5 — Publication completion

Track separately:
- arXiv public preprint status;
- journal submission status;
- revisions;
- independent feedback;
- editorial/referee outcomes;
- acceptance/publication status.

Do not conflate any two.
