## Phase 4 — Mathematics

**Status:** OPEN after Gate-3 PASS in P3-S008 for selected CAND-01. PA-0001 remains UNRESOLVED_UNDER_INSPECTED_EVIDENCE; this authorization is not a novelty finding. P4-S001 settles k=1. P4-S002 and P4-S003 isolate the k=2 inverse-list/sheet boundary. P4-S004 isolates the low conditional-weight stopping problem. P4-S005 proves that k=2 does not make those hitting measures computable and blocks a natural symmetric fibre-count lift. P4-S006 computes the canonical lexicographic sheet complexity and blocks a single rotating hidden bit. P4-S007 shows that delayed coalescence has a coherent computable width-two skeleton and forces a global freshness condition. P4-S008 rules out persistent-hole winning scan sources. P4-S009 proves that uniformly unavoidable sentinels have computably searchable deadlines and bounded deferred wagers can be hedged exactly. P4-S010 proves that threshold capping still does not force a computable exact optional projection: a global k=2 scan can encode a noncomputable c.e.-open sentinel-consumption probability into first projected values of a cap-2 martingale. An adaptive singleton-spine comb can meet the global fibre/freshness geometry. P4-S011 supplies the missing computably random source using a weak-truth-table autoreduction and converts it into an exact everywhere-total fair-coin-preserving global-k=2 scan destroyer. General k=2 forward preservation is therefore false. P4-S012 shows that the conversion needs no wtt use bound: target-visible self-avoiding partial predictions, and more generally a target-winning partial stake spine, suffice. Every computably-random singleton-spine winning global-k=2 scan has an exact self-avoiding stake functional in its original query order, but an all-correct bit-autoreduction converse is not forced by arbitrary martingale success. P4-S013 shows that reachable-sentinel totality is equivalent to computably searchable finite deadlines, makes the least-fresh scan globally exhaustive and returns it to the k=1 effective-isomorphism regime. P4-S014 moves strictly below finite deadlines: a total computable horizon selector whose exact conditional miss probabilities have one finite uniform pathwise sum budget yields a one-martingale transfer through a sentinel-first completion, miss tickets and truncated/restart hedging. Genuine infinite avoiding siblings are permitted, while P4-S011 necessarily violates this budget condition. P4-S015 answers that issue positively at the scan/martingale certificate level: a computable finite fair-price budget for leafwise majorants of the positive skipped-sentinel gain suffices, even with nonsummable raw miss probabilities. The exact minimal future-loss envelope need not be uniformly computable, and P4-S011 violates every finite certificate of this form. The next live issue is whether the advance computable envelope can be replaced by incrementally purchased loss tickets as larger postmiss stakes become finitely visible.

**Purpose:** do the actual mathematical work.

Activities may include exact definitions, theorem development, counterexamples, equivalence/separation results, characterizations, examples, computations where justified, Lean formalisation and Palomar registration.

## Phase 5 — Publication

**Purpose:** place the contribution in the public record and pursue genuine peer review.

Expected outputs include manuscript, reproducible references/claims, arXiv package, appropriate classification, venue-policy checks, disclosure statement, reviewer/outreach records where authorized, submission package and revision history.

**Endpoint:** arXiv dissemination plus a serious journal submission process, with the ultimate goal of peer-reviewed publication. Acceptance/publication is externally controlled and never presumed.

## Backtracking

Later evidence can reopen earlier gates. Prior art discovered in Phase 4 or 5 can return the programme to Phase 3 or retire the target. A mathematical failure can return it to Phase 2. Gate history must never be rewritten.
